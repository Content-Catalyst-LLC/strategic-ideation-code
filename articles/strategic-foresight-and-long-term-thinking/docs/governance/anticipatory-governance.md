# Anticipatory Governance

Anticipatory governance ensures that strategic foresight is connected to real decisions, reviewed over time, ethically grounded, and preserved as organizational learning.

## Governance requirements

- Foresight brief
- Temporal depth map
- Horizon signal register
- Driver and uncertainty matrix
- Scenario or future set
- Strategy stress-test matrix
- Path-dependence review
- Adaptive pathway map
- Futures ethics review
- Decision linkage record
- Foresight learning memory record

## Review cadence

- Monthly: horizon signals and weak-signal watchlist
- Quarterly: driver and uncertainty review, trigger conditions, adaptive pathways
- Semiannually: strategy stress tests and path-dependence review
- Annually: scenario refresh, intergenerational responsibility review, governance adequacy, and learning-memory review

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not treat foresight as prediction.
- Do not let short-term return erase long-term risk.
- Do not ignore path dependence and lock-in.
- Do not monitor signals without assigning owners.
- Do not build scenarios without connecting them to decisions.
- Do not use futures work without ethics and representation review.
- Do not revise strategy without documenting rationale and remaining uncertainty.
