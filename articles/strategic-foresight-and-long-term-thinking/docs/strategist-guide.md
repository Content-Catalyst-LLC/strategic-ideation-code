# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports strategic foresight and long-term thinking through ten recurring questions:

1. **Focal question:** What strategic decision requires a longer temporal horizon?
2. **Temporal depth:** What changes across immediate, short, medium, long, and intergenerational horizons?
3. **Signals:** What weak signals and emerging changes should be monitored?
4. **Drivers:** What technological, ecological, political, social, economic, demographic, or institutional forces matter?
5. **Uncertainty:** Which drivers are consequential and difficult to predict?
6. **Scenarios:** What plausible futures should be explored?
7. **Stress test:** How does current strategy perform across those futures?
8. **Path dependence:** What present commitments may constrain future choice?
9. **Adaptive pathways:** What triggers, options, and staged commitments preserve flexibility?
10. **Ethics and memory:** Whose futures are represented, and how will learning be preserved?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score foresight profiles

Use `foresight_profiles.csv` to compare short-term return, foresight depth, resilience, flexibility, path-dependence risk, signal capacity, scenario capacity, option value, ethics review, governance capacity, and learning memory.

### Step 2: Review horizon signals

Use `horizon_signals.csv` to evaluate weak signals by signal strength, noise risk, lead-time value, strategic relevance, interpretation quality, monitoring quality, and ownership.

### Step 3: Review drivers and uncertainties

Use `driver_uncertainties.csv` to assess impact, predictability, uncertainty, systemic interdependence, evidence quality, stakeholder salience, and monitoring feasibility.

### Step 4: Stress test strategies

Use `strategy_stress_tests.csv` to compare strategic performance across plausible futures.

### Step 5: Map path dependence

Use `path_dependence.csv` to evaluate lock-in strength, reversibility, transition cost, capability gaps, governance constraints, stakeholder constraints, and option-loss risk.

### Step 6: Design adaptive pathways

Use `adaptive_pathways.csv` to evaluate trigger clarity, reversibility, option value, resource flexibility, capability readiness, governance clarity, stakeholder alignment, and learning memory.

### Step 7: Review anticipatory governance

Use `anticipatory_governance.csv` to evaluate decision rights, evidence standards, review cadence, signal ownership, trigger conditions, ethics review, documentation, and accountability.

### Step 8: Review futures ethics

Use `futures_ethics.csv` to evaluate representation, power, burden shifts, intergenerational responsibility, accessibility, accountability, and redress.

### Step 9: Preserve foresight memory

Use `foresight_learning_memory.csv` to record focal questions, signals, drivers, uncertainties, scenarios, decisions, pathways, remaining uncertainty, and reuse value.
