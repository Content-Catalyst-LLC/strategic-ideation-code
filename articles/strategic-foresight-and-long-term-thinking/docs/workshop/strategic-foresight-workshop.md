# Strategic Foresight and Long-Term Thinking Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team use foresight to widen temporal depth, interpret weak signals, stress test strategy, reduce lock-in, and design adaptive pathways.

## Suggested duration

3 to 4 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Foresight or futures facilitator
- Systems thinker
- Data or evidence lead
- Product, service, policy, or program owner
- Operations representative
- Governance or risk lead
- Stakeholder or community representative
- Ethics reviewer
- Decision owner
- Learning-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Define the focal question

Clarify the strategic decision, uncertainty, or system that requires long-term thinking.

### 2. Map time horizons

Compare the decision across immediate, short, medium, long, and intergenerational horizons.

### 3. Scan signals

Identify weak signals, anomalies, emerging behaviors, policy experiments, technological niches, and cultural shifts.

### 4. Map drivers

Identify technological, ecological, political, social, economic, demographic, institutional, and infrastructural forces.

### 5. Select critical uncertainties

Prioritize uncertainties by impact and unpredictability.

### 6. Stress test strategy

Evaluate current and proposed strategies across plausible futures.

### 7. Review path dependence

Identify lock-in, transition cost, capability gaps, and option-loss risks.

### 8. Design adaptive pathways

Create trigger conditions, staged commitments, modular options, and decision points.

### 9. Review ethics and representation

Ask whose futures are represented, who bears future burden, and how intergenerational consequences are considered.

### 10. Create foresight memory

Document signals, drivers, scenarios, decisions, pathways, assumptions, and remaining uncertainty.

## Output

- foresight brief
- temporal depth map
- signal register
- driver and uncertainty map
- strategy stress-test matrix
- path-dependence review
- adaptive pathway map
- anticipatory governance record
- futures ethics review
- foresight learning memory record
