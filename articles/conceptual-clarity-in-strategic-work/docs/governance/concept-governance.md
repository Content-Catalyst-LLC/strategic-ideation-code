# Concept Governance

Strategic concepts should be governed when they guide decisions, budgets, metrics, roles, narratives, or accountability.

## Governance requirements

- Concept owner
- Contextual definition
- Boundary rules
- Distinction map
- Metric-validity review
- Stakeholder and ethical visibility review
- Revision triggers
- Documentation standard
- Decision-memory records

## Review cadence

- Monthly: review concepts involved in active strategic decisions
- Quarterly: review metrics and proxy risks
- Quarterly: check interpretation variance across teams
- Semiannual: review conceptual drift
- Annual: review concept architecture and strategic language system

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not use a strategic concept in a decision matrix unless it is defined.
- Do not treat a proxy as the concept itself.
- Do not treat agreement with a word as agreement with its meaning.
- Do not allow concepts to drift without documentation.
- Do not exclude affected stakeholders from concept review when the concept shapes burdens or benefits.
