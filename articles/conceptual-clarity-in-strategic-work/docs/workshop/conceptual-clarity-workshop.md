# Conceptual Clarity Workshop

## Purpose

Help a strategy team turn vague strategic language into definitions, boundaries, distinctions, metrics, decision implications, and governance rules.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Communications lead
- Analytics or evaluation lead
- Operations lead
- Stakeholder or ethics representative
- Knowledge architecture lead
- Facilitator
- Decision owner

## Agenda

### 1. Concept inventory

List the concepts doing the most strategic work.

### 2. Definition round

Ask each participant to define the same concept independently.

### 3. False-consensus check

Compare interpretations and identify hidden disagreement.

### 4. Boundary setting

Define what the concept includes and excludes.

### 5. Distinction mapping

Separate the concept from nearby terms.

### 6. Metric-validity review

Ask whether current metrics represent the concept or distort it.

### 7. Decision implications

Identify what changes because the concept is being used.

### 8. Governance assignment

Assign concept owner, review cadence, documentation standard, and revision triggers.

## Output

- concept inventory
- definition record
- boundary rules
- distinction map
- metric-validity review
- decision implication table
- concept governance record
