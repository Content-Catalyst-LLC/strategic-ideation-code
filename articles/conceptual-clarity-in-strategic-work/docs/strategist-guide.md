# Strategist Guide

This scaffold is designed for professional conceptual clarity work. It supports eight recurring strategy questions:

1. **Inventory:** Which concepts are doing strategic work?
2. **Definition:** What does each concept mean in this context?
3. **Boundary:** What does each concept include and exclude?
4. **Distinction:** Which nearby concepts must be separated?
5. **Interpretation:** Do different groups understand the concept differently?
6. **Measurement:** Do the metrics represent the concept or distort it?
7. **Drift:** Has the concept changed meaning over time?
8. **Governance:** Who owns the concept and when should it be revised?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score the concept inventory

Use `concept_inventory.csv` and the Python workflow to assess definition clarity, boundary clarity, distinction quality, operational implication, measurement validity, revision capacity, stakeholder visibility, ethical visibility, and governance maturity.

### Step 2: Review interpretation variance

Use `interpretation_survey.csv` to identify false consensus and actor-group disagreement.

### Step 3: Audit boundaries and distinctions

Use `concept_boundaries.csv` and `distinction_map.csv` to clarify what concepts include, exclude, and must not be confused with.

### Step 4: Review metric validity

Use `metric_register.csv` to identify proxy risk, incentive distortion, and qualitative gaps.

### Step 5: Diagnose conceptual drift

Use `drift_events.csv` to detect narrowing, expansion, politicization, operational flattening, proxy substitution, and messaging dilution.

### Step 6: Strengthen governance

Use `governance_reviews.csv` to assign owners, review cadences, revision triggers, and stakeholder review requirements.

## Professional facilitation questions

- What concept is doing the most work in this decision?
- What does the concept mean here, not in general?
- What does the concept exclude?
- Which related concept is being confused with it?
- What behavior does the metric incentivize?
- Who benefits from keeping the concept vague?
- What evidence would require the concept to be revised?
