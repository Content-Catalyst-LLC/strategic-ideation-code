# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports idea-to-strategy conversion through ten recurring questions:

1. **Purpose:** Why does this idea matter?
2. **Fit:** Does it belong in the strategic direction?
3. **Evidence:** What is known and what is assumed?
4. **Feasibility:** Can it be implemented?
5. **Viability:** Can it be sustained?
6. **Integration:** Can the system absorb it?
7. **Commitment:** What resources will be allocated?
8. **Alignment:** Can the organization coordinate around it?
9. **Learning:** How will implementation generate feedback?
10. **Ethics:** Who benefits, who bears burden, and who decides?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Build the initiative inventory

Use `initiatives.csv` to compare candidate ideas and pathways.

### Step 2: Review strategic fit

Use `strategic_fit.csv` to examine purpose, objectives, portfolio fit, capability fit, narrative coherence, and opportunity cost.

### Step 3: Assess integration readiness

Use `integration_readiness.csv` to test whether the organization can absorb the initiative.

### Step 4: Review resources

Use `resource_commitment.csv` to distinguish aspiration from real commitment.

### Step 5: Align and coordinate

Use `alignment_coordination.csv` to evaluate roles, incentives, decision rights, and cross-functional movement.

### Step 6: Build feedback loops

Use `feedback_learning.csv` to design monitoring, revision triggers, drift detection, and after-action learning.

### Step 7: Review ethics and power

Use `ethics_power.csv` to examine benefits, burdens, stakeholder voice, transparency, and redress.

### Step 8: Govern and remember

Use `governance_review.csv` and `decision_memory.csv` to document decisions, evidence, tradeoffs, revision triggers, and reuse.
