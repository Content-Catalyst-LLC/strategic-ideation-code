# Strategy Conversion Governance

Strategy conversion governance prevents promising ideas from becoming underdeveloped commitments.

## Governance requirements

- idea statement
- strategic purpose note
- evidence-confidence record
- alternative comparison
- tradeoff map
- capability and feasibility review
- integration map
- resource commitment plan
- alignment and coordination plan
- feedback and learning protocol
- ethics and power review
- decision gates and stop rules
- decision memory record

## Review cadence

- Before commitment: purpose, evidence, alternatives, feasibility, ethics, and fit
- During design: integration, resources, alignment, governance, and indicators
- During execution: feedback, drift detection, adaptation, and after-action learning
- After completion or exit: decision memory, lessons, and reuse

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not confuse an attractive idea with a strategy.
- Do not resource a strategy without integration design.
- Do not ignore opportunity cost.
- Do not use strategy language to hide weak evidence.
- Do not convert ideas into commitments without ethics review.
- Do not execute without feedback loops.
- Do not adapt so much that coherence disappears.
- Do not forget decision memory.
