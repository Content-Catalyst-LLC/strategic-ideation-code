# Idea-to-Strategy Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team convert a promising idea into a coherent, resourced, governable, and adaptive strategy.

## Suggested duration

3 to 4 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Idea sponsor
- Implementation lead
- Finance or resource lead
- Operations or systems owner
- Communications lead
- Stakeholder representative
- Ethics reviewer
- Risk/governance representative
- Decision-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Name the idea

State the idea precisely and identify the problem, opportunity, and strategic reason.

### 2. Define strategic purpose

Clarify why the idea matters and what future it supports.

### 3. Review evidence and assumptions

Separate evidence from belief, preference, analogy, and aspiration.

### 4. Compare alternatives

Evaluate whether the idea is the best pathway, one option in a portfolio, or an exploratory learning path.

### 5. Assess feasibility, viability, and desirability

Determine whether the idea can be implemented, sustained, and valued by affected stakeholders.

### 6. Map integration requirements

Identify processes, technologies, roles, governance systems, culture, data, dependencies, and stakeholders affected.

### 7. Define resource commitment

Specify budget, people, authority, leadership attention, communication, and technical capacity.

### 8. Align execution

Clarify roles, incentives, decision rights, cross-functional coordination, and communication.

### 9. Design learning and adaptation

Define indicators, feedback loops, revision triggers, stop rules, and drift detection.

### 10. Record the decision

Document the final judgment, tradeoffs, dissent, ethics review, and conditions that should reopen the decision.

## Output

- idea statement
- strategic purpose note
- evidence-confidence record
- tradeoff map
- capability and feasibility review
- integration map
- resource commitment plan
- alignment plan
- feedback and learning protocol
- ethics and power review
- decision memory record
