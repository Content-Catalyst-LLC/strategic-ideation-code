# Modeling Principles for From Ideas to Strategy

## 1. Ideas are not strategies

An idea becomes strategic only when it is selected, structured, resourced, governed, and connected to coordinated action.

## 2. Strategy is selective

Strategy requires tradeoffs. A process that keeps every idea alive usually creates diffusion rather than direction.

## 3. Feasibility, viability, and desirability must interact

A feasible idea may still be strategically weak. A desirable idea may need capability building. A viable idea may still be ethically fragile.

## 4. Integration is central

Many ideas fail because the receiving system is not prepared to absorb them.

## 5. Resource allocation reveals real strategy

Strategy is visible in budgets, people, attention, authority, infrastructure, and political capital.

## 6. Alignment is not automatic

Strategic direction must be translated into roles, incentives, communication, decision rights, and cross-functional coordination.

## 7. Execution must generate learning

Implementation should produce evidence that revises assumptions, detects drift, and informs future strategy.

## 8. Governance prevents aspiration drift

Decision gates, evidence standards, stop rules, escalation paths, and memory records keep strategic commitments accountable.

## 9. Ethics shape commitment

Turning an idea into strategy distributes benefits, burdens, voice, and power. Ethics is part of strategy design.

## 10. Strategy is adaptive coherence

A good strategy can change without dissolving into incoherence.
