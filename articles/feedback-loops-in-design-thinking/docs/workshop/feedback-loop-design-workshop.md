# Feedback-Loop Design Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team convert feedback from scattered signals into a responsible learning system.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Design researcher
- Product or service owner
- Data/measurement lead
- Frontline or support representative
- Accessibility reviewer
- Ethics or governance reviewer
- Systems thinker
- Decision-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Define the learning question

Name the uncertainty, assumption, design issue, implementation concern, or system effect being studied.

### 2. Inventory signal sources

List user interviews, observations, surveys, analytics, support logs, accessibility reviews, frontline notes, community input, and operational metrics.

### 3. Assess signal quality

Review relevance, timeliness, reliability, representativeness, interpretability, behavioral richness, and bias risk.

### 4. Identify missing voices

Include non-users, early abandoners, marginalized users, affected communities, frontline workers, and future users where relevant.

### 5. Define interpretation practice

Specify who interprets feedback, which perspectives are required, and how patterns are distinguished from noise.

### 6. Connect feedback to decisions

Name decision owners, revision triggers, stop rules, escalation pathways, and resource implications.

### 7. Review temporal dynamics

Consider delayed consequences, drift, accumulated friction, novelty effects, and overreaction risk.

### 8. Review systems effects

Look for feedback loops, incentives, capacity, burden shifts, scale conditions, and unintended consequences.

### 9. Conduct ethical review

Assess privacy, consent, accessibility, burden, representation, redress, accountability, and governance traceability.

### 10. Create a feedback memory record

Document what was learned, what changed, what did not change, why, and what uncertainty remains.

## Output

- learning question statement
- signal inventory
- signal quality review
- missing voices plan
- interpretation protocol
- decision pathway
- temporal learning plan
- systems-impact review
- ethical feedback review
- feedback memory record
