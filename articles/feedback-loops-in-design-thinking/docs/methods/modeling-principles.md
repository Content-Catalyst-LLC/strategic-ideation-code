# Modeling Principles for Feedback Loops in Design Thinking

## 1. Treat feedback as a loop, not a pile of comments

A loop requires signal generation, interpretation, adjustment, and learning memory.

## 2. Start with the learning question

Do not collect feedback until the uncertainty, assumption, design issue, or system concern is clear.

## 3. Evaluate signal quality

Feedback must be relevant, timely, reliable, representative, interpretable, and rich enough to inform decisions.

## 4. Interpret before reacting

Fast response without interpretation creates noisy churn.

## 5. Connect feedback to authority

A loop is incomplete if feedback cannot change a design, service, policy, prototype, or strategic decision.

## 6. Interpret feedback systemically

Signals should be assessed in relation to feedback loops, delays, burden shifts, incentives, capacity, and scale.

## 7. Govern feedback ethically

Feedback collection must respect privacy, consent, accessibility, dignity, representation, redress, and accountability.

## 8. Preserve learning

Feedback should leave a record of signals, interpretations, decisions, changes, and remaining uncertainty.
