# Feedback-Loop Governance

Feedback governance ensures that feedback loops support responsible strategic learning rather than noisy churn, performative responsiveness, or extractive data collection.

## Governance requirements

- Learning question
- Signal inventory
- Signal quality review
- Missing voices review
- Interpretation protocol
- Decision and adjustment pathway
- Temporal learning plan
- Systems-impact review
- Ethical feedback review
- Feedback memory record

## Review cadence

- Before feedback collection: define learning question, signal sources, ethics, and missing voices
- During prototype or pilot: monitor signals, noise, delays, and user experience
- Before adjustment: review interpretation, systems effects, ethics, and decision authority
- After adjustment: document change, rationale, communication, and remaining uncertainty
- Before scale: review drift, burden shifts, accessibility, capacity, and governance traceability

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not collect feedback without a learning question.
- Do not confuse more feedback with better learning.
- Do not react to every signal without interpretation.
- Do not ignore non-users, early abandoners, and marginalized groups.
- Do not let feedback become a legitimacy ritual.
- Do not optimize metrics detached from purpose.
- Do not extract feedback without accountability or response.
- Do not let learning disappear into scattered notes.
