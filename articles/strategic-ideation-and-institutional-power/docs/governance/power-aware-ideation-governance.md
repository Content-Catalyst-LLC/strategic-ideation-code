# Power-Aware Ideation Governance

Power-aware governance makes institutional influence visible and reviewable before ideas become strategy.

## Requirements

- agenda-setting review
- feasibility and constraint audit
- sponsor and gatekeeper map
- evidence parity matrix
- participation influence register
- dissent and counterevidence log
- power-aware taxonomy review
- resource allocation review
- institutional memory and forgetting audit
- AI mediation log
- decision-memory requirements

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not assume the best idea naturally wins.
- Do not let feasibility labels end inquiry before constraints are tested.
- Do not give power-aligned ideas lower evidence standards.
- Do not treat participation as influence unless participants can affect decisions.
- Do not classify dissent as resistance without analysis.
- Do not let AI-generated fluency polish institutional preference into strategy.
