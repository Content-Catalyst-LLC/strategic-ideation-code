# Institutional Power and Strategic Ideation Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team examine whether strategic ideation is judging ideas on merit and responsibility or reproducing existing institutional preferences.

## Suggested duration

3 to 4 hours.

## Outputs

- agenda-setting map
- feasibility and constraint review
- sponsor and gatekeeper map
- evidence parity matrix
- participation influence register
- dissent and counterevidence log
- power-aware taxonomy review
- resource allocation map
- memory and forgetting audit
- AI mediation log
