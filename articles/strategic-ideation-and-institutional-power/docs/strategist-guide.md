# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports institutional power analysis in strategic ideation through ten recurring questions:

1. Who set the agenda, and what was excluded?
2. Who defines realism, feasibility, scope, and political viability?
3. Which ideas have sponsors, gatekeepers, funding, and decision access?
4. Are evidence standards applied consistently?
5. Who participates visibly, and who has actual influence?
6. Are dissent, warnings, and counterideas protected?
7. Do taxonomy categories make burden, harm, and stakeholder meaning visible?
8. Which ideas receive resources, capacity, and implementation support?
9. What failures, objections, and prior lessons are remembered or forgotten?
10. How did AI mediate generation, classification, retrieval, or communication?

Recommended workflow:

- Run `python3 python/institutional_power_diagnostics.py`
- Review `outputs/tables/power_idea_scores.csv`
- Review `outputs/tables/agenda_power_scores.csv`
- Review `outputs/tables/evidence_parity_scores.csv`
- Review `outputs/tables/participation_voice_scores.csv`
- Review `outputs/tables/resource_memory_ai_scores.csv`
- Use the generated markdown report for workshop discussion.
