# Opportunity Governance

Opportunity governance prevents attractive possibility from becoming unmanaged commitment.

## Governance requirements

- opportunity statement
- signal-quality review
- capability alignment review
- timing assessment
- false-positive and false-negative risk review
- ethics and power review
- learning pathway and smallest responsible test
- evidence thresholds
- decision gates
- stop rules
- decision memory

## Review cadence

- Monthly: active tests and evidence updates
- Quarterly: portfolio review, timing windows, capability gaps, ethics review
- Semiannually: opportunity inventory, false-negative risk, signal scanning
- Annually: opportunity governance redesign and strategic fit review

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not confuse hype with signal.
- Do not scale without capability.
- Do not reject unfamiliar opportunities only because they stretch current capacity.
- Do not ignore timing.
- Do not treat recognition as commitment.
- Do not hide power behind opportunity language.
- Do not pursue opportunities without stop rules.
- Do not forget decision memory.
