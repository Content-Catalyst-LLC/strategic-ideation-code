# Opportunity Evaluation Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team move from an attractive possibility to a disciplined opportunity evaluation.

## Suggested duration

3 to 4 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Opportunity sponsor
- Domain expert
- Implementation lead
- Finance or resource lead
- Stakeholder representative
- Ethics reviewer
- Risk or governance representative
- Challenge-role participant
- Decision-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Name the opportunity

State the opportunity clearly: condition, need, actor, value pathway, and strategic reason.

### 2. Separate signal from story

Identify evidence sources, noise, hype, assumptions, and independent confirmation.

### 3. Assess capability

Review skills, assets, authority, partnerships, governance, implementation capacity, and credibility.

### 4. Evaluate value

Assess desirability, viability, strategic fit, learning value, and option value.

### 5. Review timing

Determine whether the opportunity is early, emerging, timely, late, premature, or expired.

### 6. Map risk and recognition error

Evaluate false-positive risk, missed-opportunity risk, strategic distraction, opportunity cost, and uncertainty.

### 7. Review ethics and power

Ask who defines the opportunity, who benefits, who bears burden, whose evidence counts, and what harms are unacceptable.

### 8. Define the learning pathway

Identify the smallest responsible test, evidence thresholds, decision gates, and stop rules.

### 9. Record the decision

Document assumptions, evidence, dissent, decision logic, and revision triggers.

## Output

- opportunity statement
- signal evidence note
- capability alignment review
- timing analysis
- risk and recognition-error review
- ethics and power review
- learning agenda
- decision-gate record
- decision memory record
