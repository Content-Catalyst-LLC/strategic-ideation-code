# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports opportunity recognition and evaluation through ten recurring questions:

1. **Signal:** What evidence suggests this opportunity is real?
2. **Need:** What problem, gap, pressure, or unmet demand does it address?
3. **Capability:** Can the organization act on it?
4. **Desirability:** Who values this opportunity and why?
5. **Viability:** Can it be sustained?
6. **Timing:** Is it early, timely, late, or premature?
7. **Risk:** What could go wrong?
8. **Learning:** What uncertainty can a small test reduce?
9. **Ethics:** Who benefits, who bears burden, and who decides?
10. **Governance:** How will the opportunity advance, pause, revise, or stop?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Build the opportunity inventory

Use `opportunities.csv` to define opportunity profiles.

### Step 2: Assess signal quality

Use `signal_quality.csv` to distinguish credible signals from hype.

### Step 3: Review capability alignment

Use `capability_alignment.csv` to evaluate whether the organization can act.

### Step 4: Review timing

Use `timing_windows.csv` to identify readiness, urgency, and premature pressure.

### Step 5: Evaluate recognition errors

Use `risk_errors.csv` to compare false-positive and false-negative risk.

### Step 6: Review ethics and power

Use `ethics_power.csv` to evaluate stakeholder voice, burden, transparency, and redress.

### Step 7: Define learning pathways

Use `learning_pathways.csv` to design the smallest responsible test.

### Step 8: Govern and remember

Use `governance_review.csv` and `decision_memory.csv` to document decisions and revision triggers.
