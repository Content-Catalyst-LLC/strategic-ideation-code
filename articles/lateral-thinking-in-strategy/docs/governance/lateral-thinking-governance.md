# Lateral Thinking Governance

A professional strategic ideation system should govern how frames are named, assumptions are challenged, lateral moves are generated, reframes are evaluated, and decisions are remembered.

## Governance requirements

- Dominant-frame inventory
- Assumption register
- Lateral-move log
- Reframed problem statements
- Systems-fit review
- Stakeholder legitimacy review
- Political-safety review
- Convergence-integration record
- Prototype and evidence plan
- Decision-memory archive

## Review cadence

- Before ideation: define the dominant frame
- During exploration: protect lateral moves from premature judgment
- After disruption: reconstruct usable problem statements
- Before convergence: test systems fit and stakeholder legitimacy
- Before implementation: define evidence and prototype pathways
- After decision: record rejected, deferred, and advanced reframes

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not treat novelty as value by itself.
- Do not evaluate lateral moves only by the old frame.
- Do not bypass ethical, ecological, or stakeholder constraints.
- Do not let workshops substitute for governance change.
- Do not lose rejected reframes that may become useful later.
