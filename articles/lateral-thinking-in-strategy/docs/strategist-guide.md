# Strategist Guide

This scaffold is designed for professional lateral-thinking strategy work. It supports nine recurring questions:

1. **Dominant frame:** What problem do we think we are solving?
2. **Frame origin:** Where did this way of seeing come from?
3. **Assumptions:** What must be true for the current frame to hold?
4. **Lateral move:** Which technique disrupts the frame?
5. **Reconstruction:** What new problem form emerges?
6. **Systems fit:** Does the reframe hold under feedback, constraints, incentives, and second-order effects?
7. **Legitimacy:** Do affected stakeholders recognize the reframe as meaningful?
8. **Convergence:** Can the lateral idea become testable and actionable?
9. **Memory:** Why was the reframe advanced, revised, rejected, or deferred?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score the lateral context

Use `lateral_contexts.csv` and the Python workflow to assess frame rigidity, provocation, analogical distance, random entry, reversal, challenge, convergence discipline, systems integration, stakeholder legitimacy, political safety, and transformational potential.

### Step 2: Review the dominant frame

Use `dominant_frames.csv` to evaluate problem clarity, assumption visibility, metric lock-in, category rigidity, stakeholder exclusion, system-boundary quality, and obsolescence risk.

### Step 3: Challenge assumptions

Use `assumption_register.csv` to determine which assumptions deserve challenge, testing, or power review.

### Step 4: Score lateral moves

Use `lateral_moves.csv` to evaluate whether provocations, reversals, random entries, forced associations, and escapes have strategic relevance and reconstruction quality.

### Step 5: Review reframed problems

Use `reframed_problems.csv` to assess clarity, structural shift, stakeholder visibility, systems alignment, decision value, testability, and implementation pathway quality.

### Step 6: Integrate with convergence

Use `convergence_integration.csv` to test whether reframes can move through evidence, prototypes, risk review, stakeholder review, systems review, implementation, and decision memory.

### Step 7: Review legitimacy

Use `stakeholder_legitimacy.csv` to identify political-safety and burden-visibility gaps.
