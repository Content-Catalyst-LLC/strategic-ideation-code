# Modeling Principles for Lateral Thinking in Strategy

## 1. Treat lateral thinking as frame disruption

Lateral thinking is not just idea volume. It changes the representation of the problem.

## 2. Name the dominant frame

Before reframing, define the current problem frame, its origin, its metrics, its categories, and its exclusions.

## 3. Separate disruption from reconstruction

Provocation, reversal, random entry, escape, and challenge create discontinuity. Strategic value appears only after reconstruction.

## 4. Penalize drift

A lateral move can be novel but strategically weak if it lacks evidence pathways, stakeholder fit, systems fit, or convergence integration.

## 5. Test reframed problems

A useful reframe should improve clarity, reveal structure, increase decision value, and become testable.

## 6. Protect political safety

If constructive challenge is unsafe, lateral thinking will avoid the real frame.

## 7. Integrate with convergence

Lateral thinking expands or changes the search space. Convergence selects, tests, and translates.

## 8. Preserve decision memory

Rejected and deferred reframes can become valuable later if conditions change.
