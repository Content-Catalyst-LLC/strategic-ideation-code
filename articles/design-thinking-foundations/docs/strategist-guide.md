# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports design thinking foundations through ten recurring questions:

1. **Inquiry:** What have we learned from lived experience?
2. **Framing:** How has the problem definition changed?
3. **Divergence:** Did we widen the option space enough?
4. **Convergence:** Did we narrow the option space using evidence and strategic fit?
5. **Prototype:** What assumption does each prototype test?
6. **Testing:** What evidence did testing actually produce?
7. **Systems:** What feedback, delay, burden shift, or unintended consequence may occur?
8. **Ethics:** Who benefits, who bears burden, and who has power?
9. **Decision linkage:** How does design evidence influence decisions and resources?
10. **Institutional memory:** How will learning be preserved and reused?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score design capability

Use `design_contexts.csv` to compare empathy depth, reframing capacity, prototyping, testing, systems awareness, ethical review, decision linkage, adaptability, and institutional memory.

### Step 2: Review stakeholder inquiry

Use `stakeholder_inquiry.csv` to assess whether inquiry is deep enough to support problem framing.

### Step 3: Review reframing

Use `problem_frames.csv` to evaluate whether inquiry meaningfully changed the strategic question.

### Step 4: Review idea portfolio quality

Use `idea_portfolios.csv` to assess novelty, strategic fit, human-centered fit, evidence, systems awareness, ethics, and learning value.

### Step 5: Review prototype learning

Use `prototypes.csv` to connect prototypes to assumptions, evidence, and decisions.

### Step 6: Review evidence quality

Use `test_evidence.csv` to distinguish preference, usability, behavior, implementation, system, and outcome evidence.

### Step 7: Review systems effects

Use `systems_design.csv` to evaluate feedback, delay, burden shift, incentives, metric gaming, and monitoring quality.

### Step 8: Review ethical design

Use `ethical_design.csv` to assess participation, power, burden, consent, representation, redress, traceability, and harm monitoring.

### Step 9: Review decision linkage

Use `decision_linkage.csv` to test whether design artifacts influence authority, resources, revision triggers, and implementation pathways.

### Step 10: Preserve institutional learning

Use `institutional_learning.csv` to build design memory and reuse.
