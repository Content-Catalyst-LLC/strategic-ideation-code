# Design Thinking Governance

Design thinking governance ensures that human-centered inquiry influences strategic decisions rather than becoming superficial workshop culture.

## Governance requirements

- Problem frame and boundary statement
- Stakeholder inquiry record
- Reframing evidence
- Idea portfolio
- Prototype-test plan
- Evidence interpretation note
- Systems design review
- Ethical design review
- Decision linkage map
- Revision triggers
- Design learning record

## Review cadence

- Before ideation: review stakeholder inquiry quality
- Before prototyping: define assumptions to test
- Before pilot: review evidence and systems risks
- Before scale: review ethical risks, burden shifts, and transferability
- During implementation: monitor feedback and adoption
- After implementation: archive design learning

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not treat workshops as evidence.
- Do not call empathy complete without lived-experience inquiry.
- Do not prototype without naming the assumption being tested.
- Do not claim behavior change from satisfaction feedback.
- Do not ignore systems effects.
- Do not consult stakeholders without decision influence.
- Do not let design artifacts die outside decision-making.
