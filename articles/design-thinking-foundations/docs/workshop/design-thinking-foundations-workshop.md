# Design Thinking Foundations Workshop

## Purpose

Help a strategy team use design thinking as disciplined inquiry rather than superficial workshop theater.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Design research lead
- Stakeholder or user researcher
- Systems thinker
- Implementation owner
- Ethics or governance reviewer
- Decision-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Clarify the current problem frame

Identify who framed the problem and what assumptions are embedded in it.

### 2. Review stakeholder evidence

Assess what is known from lived experience, observation, journey mapping, contextual inquiry, and frontline knowledge.

### 3. Reframe the problem

Ask how inquiry changes the strategic question.

### 4. Generate options

Use divergent thinking to widen the option space, then converge using evidence, strategic fit, and ethical review.

### 5. Design prototypes

Define what each prototype will test and what decision it will inform.

### 6. Test evidence

Distinguish comprehension, preference, usability, behavior, implementation, system, and outcome evidence.

### 7. Add systems review

Map feedback loops, burden shifts, delays, incentives, metric gaming, and context dependency.

### 8. Add ethical review

Ask who benefits, who bears burden, who participates, who has power, and what redress exists.

### 9. Connect to decisions

Define how design evidence will affect priorities, resources, governance, and implementation.

### 10. Archive learning

Preserve observations, frames, assumptions, prototypes, tests, revisions, decisions, and learning records.

## Output

- problem frame and reframe
- stakeholder inquiry summary
- idea portfolio
- prototype-test plan
- systems design review
- ethical design review
- decision linkage map
- institutional learning record
