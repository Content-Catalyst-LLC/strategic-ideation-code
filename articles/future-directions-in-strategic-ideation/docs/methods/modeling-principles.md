# Modeling Principles for Future Directions in Strategic Ideation

1. Future-ready ideation is a system, not a brainstorming event.
2. Ideas should be connected to problem frames, scenarios, evidence, stakeholders, options, governance, implementation pathways, and learning records.
3. AI can expand inquiry, but human judgment remains responsible for evidence, ethics, and accountability.
4. Scenario-linked ideation tests ideas before uncertainty tests the organization.
5. Option portfolios preserve flexibility under uncertainty.
6. Collective intelligence requires influence design, not merely participation volume.
7. Ethical visibility is part of idea quality.
8. Knowledge architecture turns ideation into institutional memory.
9. Sustainability and systems responsibility require long-horizon and feedback-loop review.
10. Future-ready ideas need stop rules, revision triggers, and learning loops before implementation.
