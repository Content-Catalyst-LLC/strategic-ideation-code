# Future-Ready Strategic Ideation Governance

Future-ready ideation governance keeps creativity connected to evidence, ethics, uncertainty, stakeholders, implementation, and memory.

## Requirements

- structured idea inventory
- strategic idea taxonomy
- scenario-linked idea matrix
- option portfolio register
- AI-assisted ideation governance log
- stakeholder participation and influence map
- claim-evidence matrix
- ethical idea audit
- experimentation and assumption-test plan
- decision-memory record
- stop-rule and revision-trigger register
- periodic portfolio review cadence

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not treat more ideas as better strategy.
- Do not let AI-generated fluency substitute for evidence.
- Do not evaluate ideas against only one expected future.
- Do not select a single option too early under uncertainty.
- Do not use participation without influence.
- Do not scale an idea without learning gates and stop rules.
- Do not allow strategic memory to disappear into slides, chats, or individual recollection.
