# Future-Ready Strategic Ideation Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team move from idea generation to a future-ready strategic ideation system.

## Suggested duration

3 to 4 hours.

## Outputs

- future-ready idea inventory
- scenario-linked idea matrix
- option portfolio map
- AI governance log
- collective intelligence and participation map
- ethical idea audit
- knowledge architecture and decision-memory plan
- experimentation and assumption-test plan
- stop-rule and revision-trigger register
