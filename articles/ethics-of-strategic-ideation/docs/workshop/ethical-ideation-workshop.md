# Ethical Strategic Ideation Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team evaluate whether strategic ideas are responsibly framed, evidence-aware, participatory, accountable, reversible where needed, and prepared for ethical decision-making.

## Suggested duration

3 to 4 hours.

## Outputs

- problem-frame ethics review
- stakeholder impact map
- participation influence matrix
- claim-evidence register
- burden and distributional impact table
- bias and missing knowledge audit
- uncertainty and reversibility review
- future-generation responsibility review
- AI-use and verification log
- accountability and redress plan
