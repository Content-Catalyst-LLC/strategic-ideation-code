# Ethical Ideation Governance

Ethical ideation governance keeps responsibility visible before ideas become strategy.

## Requirements

- problem-frame ethics review
- stakeholder impact register
- participation influence matrix
- claim-evidence register
- dissent and counterevidence log
- burden and distributional impact table
- uncertainty, reversibility, and stop-rule review
- long-term responsibility review
- AI-use disclosure and verification log
- accountability and redress plan

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not treat ethics as a downstream implementation review only.
- Do not use stakeholder participation language without influence clarity.
- Do not allow claims to exceed evidence.
- Do not hide burden under efficiency, innovation, or transformation language.
- Do not let AI-generated fluency substitute for judgment.
- Do not proceed without accountability, monitoring, and redress for high-risk ideas.
