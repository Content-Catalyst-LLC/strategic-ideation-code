# Modeling Principles for First Principles Thinking in Strategy

1. Distinguish assumptions from constraints.
2. Treat inherited arrangements as hypotheses.
3. Decompose before reconstructing.
4. Identify mechanisms, not only symptoms.
5. Test rebuilt strategies against reality.
6. Avoid reductionism: legitimacy, history, identity, trust, and power may be part of the deep structure.
7. Preserve decision memory: record assumptions removed, constraints respected, and options rejected.
