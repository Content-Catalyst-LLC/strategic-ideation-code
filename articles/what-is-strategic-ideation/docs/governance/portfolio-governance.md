# Idea Portfolio Governance

A professional strategic ideation system should include governance rules for how ideas enter, move through, and leave the portfolio.

## Portfolio states

- Captured
- Framed
- Structured
- Evaluated
- Prototype planned
- Evidence reviewed
- Advanced to strategy
- Paused
- Reframed
- Retired

## Review cadence

- Weekly: capture and classify new ideas
- Monthly: review assumptions, evidence, and prototypes
- Quarterly: portfolio balance and strategic fit review
- Semiannual: decision-memory and learning-loop review

## Decision rights

Define who can:

- add ideas
- reframe ideas
- score ideas
- approve prototypes
- advance ideas into strategy
- retire ideas
- reopen rejected alternatives

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not advance high-uncertainty ideas without assumption testing.
- Do not score ideas without explicit criteria.
- Do not retire ideas without recording rationale.
- Do not treat metrics as a substitute for judgment.
- Do not treat stakeholder review as symbolic.
