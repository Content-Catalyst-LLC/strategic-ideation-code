# Strategic Ideation Workshop

## Purpose

Help a strategy team move from raw ideas to structured option architecture, testable assumptions, and strategic evaluation.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Domain expert
- Evidence or analytics lead
- Implementation owner
- Stakeholder or ethics representative
- Facilitator
- Decision owner

## Agenda

### 1. Problem framing

Clarify the problem frame and identify alternative frames.

### 2. Idea generation

Generate multiple possible approaches without premature selection.

### 3. Option architecture

Classify ideas by role:

- immediate action
- learning probe
- prototype
- strategic filter
- governance system
- implementation bridge
- future option

### 4. Assumption mapping

For each major idea, list what must be true for it to work.

### 5. Evidence review

Separate evidence from preference, habit, politics, and confidence.

### 6. Prototype planning

Identify the smallest useful test for high-uncertainty ideas.

### 7. Strategic selection

Use criteria to decide what advances, what is tested, what is reframed, and what is rejected.

## Output

- idea portfolio
- option architecture map
- assumption register
- prototype plan
- selected strategic pathway
- decision-memory entry
