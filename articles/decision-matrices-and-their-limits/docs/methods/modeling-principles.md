# Modeling Principles for Decision Matrices and Their Limits

1. Matrices support judgment; they do not replace it.
2. Criteria selection is hidden strategy.
3. Weights are value judgments.
4. Evidence confidence matters.
5. False precision is a real risk.
6. Sensitivity testing is mandatory.
7. Some criteria are non-compensatory.
8. Scenario-specific scoring improves uncertainty handling.
9. Portfolio-aware use prevents single-winner distortion.
10. Preserve decision memory.
