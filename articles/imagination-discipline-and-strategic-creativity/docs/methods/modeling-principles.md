# Modeling Principles for Strategic Creativity

## 1. Do not reduce creativity to novelty

Novelty matters, but strategic creativity also requires relevance, coherence, mechanism clarity, stakeholder grounding, systems fit, evidence, revision, and implementation judgment.

## 2. Distinguish surface novelty from mechanism novelty

New language, tools, formats, or branding may leave the underlying strategic logic unchanged.

## 3. Treat discipline as development, not suppression

Discipline should clarify, test, and mature ideas. It should not kill early ideas with final-stage criteria too soon.

## 4. Audit constraints

Some constraints are ethical or productive. Others are inherited assumptions disguised as reality.

## 5. Ground ideas in stakeholders

Strategic creativity must account for lived experience, burden, trust, agency, legitimacy, and implementation reality.

## 6. Test systems fit

Creative ideas should be examined against feedback, incentives, dependencies, delays, adaptation, and second-order effects.

## 7. Manage ideas as a portfolio

Maintain core, exploratory, quick-learning, systems, participatory, and archived ideas.

## 8. Preserve creative decision memory

Rejected, deferred, revised, and recombined ideas should become reusable strategic learning.
