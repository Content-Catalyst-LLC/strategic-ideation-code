# Strategic Creativity Workshop

## Purpose

Help a strategy team develop ideas that are imaginative, disciplined, grounded, testable, and strategically useful.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Facilitator
- Design or innovation lead
- Stakeholder representative or researcher
- Systems thinker
- Implementation owner
- Evidence or analytics lead
- Decision-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Name the creative ambition

State what the strategy process needs to imagine differently.

### 2. Expand possibility

Generate ideas through multiple frames, analogies, futures, stakeholder perspectives, and systems prompts.

### 3. Clarify concepts

For each promising idea, define the frame, mechanism, assumption set, and intended strategic effect.

### 4. Review novelty depth

Ask what is actually new: surface, frame, mechanism, institutional structure, system leverage, or learning pathway.

### 5. Audit constraints

Separate ethical and productive constraints from inherited assumptions.

### 6. Ground in stakeholders

Review lived experience, burden, trust, agency, and legitimacy.

### 7. Test systems fit

Map feedback, incentives, dependencies, delays, boundaries, and second-order effects.

### 8. Design evidence pathways

Define low-risk learning tests and disconfirming evidence.

### 9. Decide portfolio role

Classify ideas as core, exploratory, quick-learning, systems, participatory, archive, or reframe.

### 10. Preserve creative memory

Record ideas, reasons, tests, revisions, and reopen triggers.

## Output

- idea-space map
- novelty-depth review
- mechanism map
- constraint audit
- stakeholder grounding review
- systems-fit review
- evidence pathway
- idea maturation plan
- creative portfolio decision
- decision-memory record
