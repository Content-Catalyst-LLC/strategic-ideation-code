# Strategic Creativity Governance

A professional strategic creativity system should govern how ideas are generated, clarified, challenged, tested, revised, selected, and remembered.

## Governance requirements

- Idea profile
- Novelty-depth review
- Mechanism statement
- Constraint audit
- Stakeholder grounding review
- Systems-fit review
- Evidence pathway
- Maturation stage gate
- Portfolio role
- Decision-memory record

## Review cadence

- Before ideation: define creative ambition and constraints
- During generation: protect imagination and frame diversity
- Before selection: assess novelty depth, stakeholders, systems, and evidence
- Before commitment: match investment to evidence and reversibility
- After testing: revise or archive with decision memory
- Quarterly: review idea portfolio and reopen triggers

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not confuse surface novelty with strategic creativity.
- Do not kill early ideas with final-stage criteria.
- Do not protect vague ideas from clarification.
- Do not ignore stakeholder burden.
- Do not skip systems review for creative ideas.
- Do not lose rejected or deferred ideas without preserving the reason.
