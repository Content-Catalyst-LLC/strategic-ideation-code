# Institutional Memory Stewardship

Memory stewardship keeps idea systems usable, traceable, and ethically responsible.

## Requirements

- idea-system owner
- lifecycle rules
- metadata schema
- decision-memory standard
- learning-loop update process
- retrieval testing cadence
- reuse and transfer guidance
- taxonomy and controlled vocabulary stewardship
- continuity and migration plan
- access, privacy, retention, and correction rules
- ethics and power review

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not treat saved documents as institutional memory.
- Do not let decisions proceed without rationale, alternatives, dissent, and revision triggers.
- Do not let repositories become stale archives.
- Do not let AI summaries erase uncertainty, dissent, or source context.
- Do not preserve only leadership narratives while losing frontline or stakeholder knowledge.
- Do not forget failures because they are politically inconvenient.
