# Heuristic-Aware Ideation Governance

A professional strategic ideation system should govern which shortcuts are used, how they shape the search, when they are challenged, and how search closure is justified.

## Governance requirements

- Heuristic context profile
- Anchor log
- Search-breadth scorecard
- Institutional shortcut audit
- Complexity-fit review
- Stopping-rule record
- Stakeholder visibility review
- Source-domain inventory
- Decision-memory archive

## Review cadence

- Before ideation: choose multiple starting prompts and source domains
- During generation: separate fast heuristics from closure
- Before convergence: score search breadth and stakeholder visibility
- Before decision: review stopping rules and complexity fit
- After decision: preserve rejected, deferred, and unexplored ideas
- Quarterly: audit repeated institutional shortcuts and recognition traps

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not assume fast ideas are adequate ideas.
- Do not confuse familiar with relevant.
- Do not let the first frame govern the whole search.
- Do not stop when the first workable option appears unless the context truly requires it.
- Do not use stable-system heuristics for complex adaptive systems without review.
- Do not lose rejected ideas without recording the reason and reopen trigger.
