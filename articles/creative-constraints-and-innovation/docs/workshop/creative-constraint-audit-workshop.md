# Creative Constraint Audit Workshop

## Purpose

Help a strategy team determine whether constraints are focusing innovation, suppressing it, protecting legitimacy, hiding assumptions, or creating avoidable burden.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Innovation or design lead
- Operations or implementation owner
- Technical representative
- Stakeholder or ethics representative
- Sustainability or systems representative when relevant
- Knowledge architecture lead
- Facilitator

## Agenda

### 1. Constraint inventory

List visible and invisible constraints affecting the work.

### 2. Constraint classification

Separate real, temporary, assumed, political, ecological, and ethical constraints.

### 3. Constraint function mapping

Identify what each constraint does to the search process.

### 4. Constraint calibration

Decide which constraints should be loosened during divergence and which must remain active.

### 5. Stakeholder and burden review

Ask who defines the constraint, who benefits, and who bears cost.

### 6. Dynamic review

Identify constraints likely to tighten, loosen, or become contested.

### 7. Innovation option scoring

Score options by novelty, strategic fit, constraint fit, stakeholder value, ecological responsibility, ethical legitimacy, readiness, and assumption burden.

### 8. Decision memory

Document which constraints were respected, challenged, redesigned, or converted into learning questions.

## Output

- constraint register
- classification table
- function map
- stakeholder burden review
- innovation option scores
- dynamic constraint register
- decision-memory record
