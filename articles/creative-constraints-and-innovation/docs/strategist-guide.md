# Strategist Guide

This scaffold is designed for professional creative-constraint strategy work. It supports eight recurring questions:

1. **Inventory:** What constraints shape the work?
2. **Classification:** Are they real, assumed, temporary, political, ecological, or ethical?
3. **Function:** Do they focus search, reveal tradeoffs, protect legitimacy, or suppress possibility?
4. **Calibration:** Which constraints belong in divergence, convergence, or both?
5. **Stakeholders:** Who defines the constraint and who bears the burden?
6. **Dynamics:** How might the constraint change over time?
7. **Capability:** Can the organization learn through the constraint?
8. **Memory:** Why was the constraint respected, challenged, or redesigned?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score the constraint context

Use `constraint_contexts.csv` and the Python workflow to assess resource pressure, technical rigidity, institutional rigidity, ecological boundary pressure, ethical visibility, focus, adaptive opportunity, stakeholder legitimacy, learning capacity, and readiness.

### Step 2: Classify constraints

Use `constraint_register.csv` to separate real constraints from assumed or political constraints.

### Step 3: Map constraint function

Use `constraint_function_map.csv` to determine whether constraints focus innovation, reveal tradeoffs, force recombination, protect legitimacy, produce learning, or suppress possibility.

### Step 4: Score innovation options

Use `innovation_options.csv` to evaluate strategic fit, constraint fit, adaptive value, stakeholder value, ecological responsibility, ethical legitimacy, readiness, and assumption burden.

### Step 5: Review stakeholders and burden

Use `stakeholder_constraint_review.csv` to test legitimacy and burden visibility.

### Step 6: Track change

Use `dynamic_constraints.csv` to monitor constraints that are tightening, loosening, contested, or non-negotiable.

### Step 7: Build learning architecture

Use `capability_learning.csv` to examine whether the organization can transform constraint into learning rather than strain.
