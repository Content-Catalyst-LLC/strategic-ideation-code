# Creative Constraint Governance

A professional innovation system should govern how constraints are identified, classified, challenged, respected, redesigned, and revised.

## Governance requirements

- Constraint inventory
- Real vs assumed classification
- Evidence record
- Stakeholder burden review
- Ethical and ecological constraint register
- Constraint function map
- Dynamic constraint review
- Innovation option scorecard
- Decision-memory archive
- Revision triggers

## Review cadence

- Before ideation: inventory and classify constraints
- During divergence: loosen assumed constraints while preserving ethical and ecological limits
- Before convergence: reintroduce feasibility, evidence, and capacity constraints
- During implementation: monitor constraint change and burden
- Quarterly: audit false constraints and constraint drift
- Annually: review institutional constraint architecture

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not treat inherited assumptions as real limits.
- Do not romanticize scarcity when it creates harm.
- Do not remove ethical or ecological constraints in the name of creativity.
- Do not let power define realism without review.
- Do not use constraints without decision memory.
