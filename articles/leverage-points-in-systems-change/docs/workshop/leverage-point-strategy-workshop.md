# Leverage-Point Strategy Workshop

## Purpose

Help a strategy team distinguish visible activity from structurally intelligent intervention.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Facilitator
- Systems thinker
- Implementation owner
- Stakeholder researcher
- Evidence or data lead
- Governance owner
- Decision-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Define the recurring pattern

Name the behavior the system keeps producing.

### 2. Separate symptoms from structure

Identify what appears downstream and what may be generating it upstream.

### 3. Classify proposed ideas by leverage level

Map each idea to parameter, buffer, delay, feedback, information, rule, goal, or paradigm.

### 4. Map feedback loops

Identify reinforcing loops, balancing loops, and policy resistance.

### 5. Review information flows

Ask what actors can see, when they see it, and how they use it.

### 6. Review rules and incentives

Identify what behavior is rewarded, penalized, permitted, blocked, measured, or ignored.

### 7. Diagnose the operating goal

Compare stated goals with metrics, budgets, authority, and decision rights.

### 8. Surface paradigms

Identify assumptions that make the current system seem inevitable.

### 9. Identify tipping thresholds

Find conditions where change may become self-reinforcing.

### 10. Build governance

Define early-warning indicators, revision triggers, review cadence, decision rights, and decision memory.

## Output

- recurring pattern statement
- symptom-structure map
- leverage-level map
- feedback-loop review
- information-flow audit
- rules and incentives review
- operating-goal diagnosis
- paradigm review
- threshold map
- governance and learning plan
