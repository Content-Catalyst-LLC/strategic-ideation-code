# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports leverage-point analysis through ten recurring questions:

1. **Recurring pattern:** What behavior does the system keep producing?
2. **Leverage level:** Does the intervention affect parameters, buffers, delays, feedback, information, rules, goals, or paradigms?
3. **System sensitivity:** Where does small change produce meaningful system response?
4. **Feedback:** Which reinforcing or balancing loops are changed?
5. **Information:** Who sees what differently, and when?
6. **Rules and incentives:** What behavior becomes rational inside the system?
7. **Operating goal:** What is the system actually optimizing?
8. **Paradigm:** What assumptions make the system feel natural or inevitable?
9. **Thresholds:** Is there a tipping point where change becomes self-reinforcing?
10. **Governance:** What monitoring, legitimacy, and revision capacity is required?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Classify leverage depth

Use `leverage_points.csv` to compare structural depth, feedback influence, information effect, rule power, goal alignment, and paradigm relevance.

### Step 2: Review feedback leverage

Use `feedback_leverage.csv` to identify reinforcing loops, balancing loops, policy resistance, and positive cascade potential.

### Step 3: Review information flows

Use `information_flows.csv` to assess visibility, signal quality, decision linkage, stakeholder access, context quality, gaming risk, and data burden.

### Step 4: Review rules and incentives

Use `rules_incentives.csv` to evaluate behavioral power, misalignment, countermetrics, governance readiness, and political resistance.

### Step 5: Diagnose operating goals

Use `system_goals.csv` to compare stated goals with operating goals revealed by metrics, budgets, decision rights, and tradeoff logic.

### Step 6: Surface paradigms

Use `paradigms.csv` to review assumptions, alternative frames, lock-in, evidence, coalition strength, and practice embedding.

### Step 7: Review tipping thresholds

Use `tipping_thresholds.csv` to identify where a change could become self-reinforcing.

### Step 8: Build governance and learning

Use `governance_needs.csv`, `early_warning_indicators.csv`, and `learning_loops.csv` to design monitoring, revision triggers, and decision memory.
