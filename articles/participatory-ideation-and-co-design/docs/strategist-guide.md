# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports participatory ideation and co-design through ten recurring questions:

1. **Purpose:** Why are we inviting participation?
2. **Representation:** Who is affected, powerful, burdened, excluded, or missing?
3. **Influence:** What can participants actually shape?
4. **Access:** Can affected people realistically participate?
5. **Reciprocity:** What do participants receive in return for their labor and knowledge?
6. **Power:** How might hierarchy shape who speaks, what counts, and what is decided?
7. **Knowledge integration:** How will lived experience, technical expertise, evidence, and systems analysis be combined?
8. **Conflict:** What disagreement, tradeoff, or burden shift must remain visible?
9. **Decision linkage:** How did participation change the strategy?
10. **Accountability:** How will the organization close the loop?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score participation systems

Use `participation_systems.csv` to compare representation, influence, accessibility, reciprocity, power awareness, knowledge integration, decision linkage, accountability, and learning memory.

### Step 2: Map stakeholders

Use `stakeholders.csv` to identify affected groups, implementation actors, usually excluded groups, and representation gaps.

### Step 3: Review influence boundaries

Use `influence_boundaries.csv` to clarify what participants can shape and what is constrained.

### Step 4: Review access supports

Use `accessibility_supports.csv` to evaluate language, disability access, timing, technology, compensation, care support, safety, and cultural fit.

### Step 5: Review reciprocity

Use `reciprocity.csv` to evaluate participant labor, compensation, credit, feedback return, capacity building, participant benefit, and extraction risk.

### Step 6: Review power risks

Use `power_risks.csv` to evaluate invitation bias, framing control, language barriers, dominant voices, interpretive capture, decision capture, and participant risk.

### Step 7: Review knowledge integration

Use `knowledge_integration.csv` to assess whether lived experience, technical expertise, operational knowledge, systems analysis, evidence, conflict, and synthesis are integrated.

### Step 8: Review conflict and tradeoffs

Use `conflict_tradeoffs.csv` to document disagreement, burden shifts, value tensions, and resolution transparency.

### Step 9: Review decision traceability

Use `decision_traceability.csv` to show how participation shaped decisions, prototypes, revisions, and implementation.

### Step 10: Review accountability

Use `accountability.csv` to evaluate close-the-loop quality, public response, challenge paths, monitoring, governance, memory, and trust repair.
