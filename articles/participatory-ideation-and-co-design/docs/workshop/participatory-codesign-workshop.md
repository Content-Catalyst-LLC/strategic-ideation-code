# Participatory Ideation and Co-Design Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team design participation that is meaningful, accessible, power-aware, decision-linked, and accountable.

## Suggested duration

2.5 to 3.5 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Design researcher or facilitator
- User or community representatives
- Frontline implementers
- Accessibility reviewer
- Ethics or governance reviewer
- Systems thinker
- Decision owner
- Learning-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Define participation purpose

Clarify whether participation is for problem framing, ideation, prototype design, testing, evaluation, governance, implementation, or learning.

### 2. Map affected stakeholders

Identify direct users, indirect stakeholders, implementers, excluded groups, community actors, decision authorities, technical experts, and future stakeholders.

### 3. Define influence boundaries

State what participants can shape, what is constrained, who decides, and what will happen with input.

### 4. Design access

Review language, disability access, scheduling, location, technology, compensation, care, transportation, psychological safety, and cultural fit.

### 5. Review reciprocity

Define compensation, credit, shared learning, participant benefit, and close-the-loop commitments.

### 6. Review power risks

Identify hierarchy, dominant voices, jargon, invitation bias, interpretive capture, and decision capture.

### 7. Choose methods

Select listening sessions, participatory journey mapping, co-creation workshops, design games, role-play, prototypes, review panels, or participatory evaluation.

### 8. Plan accountable synthesis

Define how input becomes assumptions, insights, criteria, prototypes, risks, tradeoffs, and decisions.

### 9. Document conflict

Record disagreement, tradeoffs, burden shifts, values conflicts, and unresolved tensions.

### 10. Close the loop

Plan how participants will receive decisions, rationale, changes, next steps, and future opportunities for challenge or involvement.

## Output

- participation purpose statement
- stakeholder and inclusion map
- influence boundary statement
- access and support plan
- reciprocity plan
- power-risk review
- method plan
- synthesis protocol
- conflict and tradeoff register
- decision traceability record
- accountability plan
