# Governance for Strategic Idea Portfolios

Portfolio governance ensures that ideas advance, pause, merge, scale, or retire through explicit criteria rather than sponsorship, habit, or political convenience.

## Governance requirements

- Idea inventory
- Portfolio role map
- Risk-learning matrix
- Time-horizon balance review
- Capacity load review
- Dependency and sequencing map
- Option-value register
- Ethics and power review
- Entry and pruning rules
- Review cadence
- Decision memory record

## Review cadence

- Monthly: active idea status, capacity load, immediate blockers
- Quarterly: role balance, evidence readiness, risk-learning matrix, dependencies
- Semiannually: ethics and power review, long-horizon balance, option value
- Annually: portfolio strategy, pruning rules, legacy initiative review, governance redesign

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not confuse an idea backlog with a strategic portfolio.
- Do not let safe incremental ideas crowd out exploration.
- Do not let transformational bets overwhelm capacity.
- Do not fund pilots without learning questions.
- Do not keep legacy ideas alive without review.
- Do not let scoring replace judgment.
- Do not ignore sponsor power, stakeholder voice, or burden distribution.
- Do not let the portfolio grow without pruning.
