# Governance for Strategic Interaction Analysis

Game-theoretic governance ensures that interdependent strategy does not become naive response prediction, manipulation, or over-formalized abstraction.

## Governance requirements

- Strategic interaction map
- Actor and payoff profile
- Strategy-set comparison
- Information and signal map
- Timing and sequencing map
- Equilibrium diagnosis
- Cooperation-fragility review
- Repeated-interaction review
- Mechanism-design review
- Behavioral game-theory adjustment
- Ethics and power review
- Strategic interaction memory record

## Review cadence

- Before major move: actor/payoff, information, equilibrium, signal, ethics review
- Monthly: signal interpretation, response monitoring, cooperation fragility
- Quarterly: mechanism design, repeated interaction, behavioral realism, learning memory
- Annually: institutional patterns in strategic interaction and rule-design effectiveness

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not assume others are passive.
- Do not assume others value what you value.
- Do not assume cooperation is stable because it is collectively beneficial.
- Do not confuse equilibrium with desirability.
- Do not use payoff models without ethics and power review.
- Do not treat signals as self-interpreting.
- Do not redesign incentives without considering legitimacy, voice, exit, and burden.
- Do not forget the response history.
