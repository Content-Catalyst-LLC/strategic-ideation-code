# Game Theory and Strategic Interaction Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team test whether a strategic idea survives interdependence: response, retaliation, imitation, coordination failure, signaling, bargaining, power, and rule design.

## Suggested duration

3 to 4 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Decision owner
- Economist or game-theory-informed analyst
- Systems thinker
- Policy or governance representative
- Product, platform, or program owner
- Stakeholder or user representative
- Ethics reviewer
- Challenge-role participant
- Learning-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Define the strategic interaction

Clarify the focal strategy, setting, boundary, and outcome of interest.

### 2. Identify players

Map actors who can act, react, block, enable, imitate, defect, coordinate, regulate, or redesign the game.

### 3. Map payoffs

Clarify material, reputational, legitimacy, risk, control, cooperation, exit, and power dimensions.

### 4. Define strategies

Identify plausible moves for each actor, including cooperation, defection, delay, imitation, escalation, coalition formation, and rule challenge.

### 5. Analyze information

Map who knows what, who observes which moves, what is hidden, and how signals may be interpreted.

### 6. Diagnose equilibrium

Ask what outcome is likely to stabilize under current incentives and whether that outcome is desirable.

### 7. Test cooperation

Review trust, monitoring, enforcement, reciprocity, reputation, and temptation to defect.

### 8. Review repeated interaction

Assess reputation, memory, future shadow, proportional retaliation, forgiveness, and trust repair.

### 9. Redesign the game

Identify rule, incentive, information, verification, participation, and governance mechanisms.

### 10. Add behavioral realism

Account for fairness, identity, bounded reasoning, trust, loss aversion, norms, and legitimacy.

### 11. Review ethics and power

Ask who bears cost, risk, constraint, manipulation, or exclusion.

### 12. Create interaction memory

Document players, payoffs, signals, equilibrium logic, mechanisms, decisions, assumptions, and revisions.

## Output

- strategic interaction map
- actor/payoff profile
- information and signal map
- equilibrium diagnosis
- cooperation fragility review
- repeated-interaction review
- mechanism-design options
- behavioral realism review
- ethics and power review
- strategic interaction learning memory
