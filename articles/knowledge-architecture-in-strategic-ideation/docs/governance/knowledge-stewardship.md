# Knowledge Stewardship

Knowledge stewardship keeps strategic knowledge systems usable, trustworthy, and ethically responsible.

## Requirements

- taxonomy owner
- metadata schema owner
- controlled vocabulary and concept-definition review
- repository curation cycle
- evidence standards
- decision-memory rules
- retrieval testing cadence
- privacy and access governance
- ethics and power review
- retirement and archival rules

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not treat repositories as storage closets.
- Do not let ideas enter the system without metadata.
- Do not separate ideas from assumptions and evidence.
- Do not let AI-generated ideation bypass evidence and governance.
- Do not erase dissent to make knowledge records cleaner.
- Do not privilege only leadership categories if affected groups experience the strategy differently.
- Do not allow taxonomies to decay without review.
