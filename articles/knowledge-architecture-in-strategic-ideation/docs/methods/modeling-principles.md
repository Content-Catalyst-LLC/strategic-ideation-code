# Modeling Principles for Knowledge Architecture in Strategic Ideation

1. Strategic ideas should be treated as reusable knowledge objects.
2. Metadata is what preserves context across teams and time.
3. Taxonomies shape what a strategy system can see.
4. Semantic coherence prevents strategic language from drifting.
5. Evidence and assumptions should be linked directly to ideas.
6. Relationship mapping supports pattern recognition and recombination.
7. Retrieval quality requires relevance, context, trust, and usability.
8. Decision memory creates traceability from idea to choice to learning.
9. Stewardship is required because knowledge systems decay.
10. Ethical knowledge design asks whose knowledge is represented, searchable, protected, or forgotten.
