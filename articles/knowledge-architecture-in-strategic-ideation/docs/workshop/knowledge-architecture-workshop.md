# Strategic Knowledge Architecture Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team determine whether strategic ideas are organized for future judgment or merely stored.

## Suggested duration

3 to 4 hours.

## Outputs

- knowledge architecture purpose statement
- strategic knowledge inventory
- idea taxonomy
- metadata schema
- concept definition and controlled vocabulary starter set
- evidence and assumption register
- relationship map or knowledge graph
- retrieval test report
- decision-memory assessment
- stewardship model
- ethics and power review
