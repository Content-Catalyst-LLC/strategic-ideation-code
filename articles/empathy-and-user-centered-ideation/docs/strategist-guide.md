# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports empathy and user-centered ideation through ten recurring questions:

1. **Experience:** What does the system feel like from within?
2. **Projection:** Where might the institution be mistaking its own logic for user reality?
3. **Stakeholders:** Who is affected, excluded, required, or burdened?
4. **Observation:** What do people actually do, avoid, repeat, or work around?
5. **Journey:** Where does friction accumulate across time?
6. **Unmet need:** What need is hidden beneath silence, workaround, or stated preference?
7. **Preference and behavior:** Where do user statements and behavior diverge?
8. **Reframing:** How does lived evidence change the problem?
9. **Ethics and systems:** Who bears burden, who has power, and what system effects may occur?
10. **Decision linkage:** How will user-centered evidence change action?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score empathy profiles

Use `empathy_contexts.csv` to compare observation depth, projection risk, unmet-need visibility, stakeholder breadth, reframing, ethics, systems awareness, decision linkage, and institutional memory.

### Step 2: Map stakeholders

Use `stakeholder_field.csv` to identify who should be included in inquiry.

### Step 3: Review observations

Use `observations.csv` to test whether evidence is behaviorally and contextually rich.

### Step 4: Score journey friction

Use `journey_friction.csv` to prioritize accumulated burden.

### Step 5: Prioritize unmet needs

Use `unmet_needs.csv` to identify needs hidden beneath visible requests.

### Step 6: Compare preference and behavior

Use `preference_behavior.csv` to identify interpretive gaps.

### Step 7: Score reframing quality

Use `problem_reframes.csv` to determine whether empathy changed the question.

### Step 8: Review ethics

Use `ethical_empathy.csv` to examine power, participation, burden, consent, representation, redress, traceability, and harm monitoring.

### Step 9: Review systems effects

Use `systems_empathy.csv` to examine feedback, delay, burden shifts, incentives, and metric gaming.

### Step 10: Connect insight to decisions

Use `decision_linkage.csv` to ensure user evidence changes action.
