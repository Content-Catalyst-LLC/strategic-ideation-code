# Empathy and User-Centered Ideation Governance

Empathy governance ensures that lived-experience evidence changes strategy rather than decorating decisions already made.

## Governance requirements

- Initial problem frame
- Stakeholder field map
- Observation and inquiry record
- Journey friction map
- Unmet-need register
- Preference-behavior comparison
- Reframed problem statement
- Ethical empathy review
- Systems empathy review
- Prototype-test plan
- Decision traceability record
- User insight memory record

## Review cadence

- Before ideation: review projection risk and stakeholder field
- Before prototyping: define the unmet need and assumption to test
- Before pilot: review journey friction, ethics, and systems effects
- Before scale: review burden shifts, decision traceability, and evidence quality
- During implementation: monitor user behavior, support signals, and hidden burden
- After implementation: archive user insight and revision decisions

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not treat personas as evidence unless grounded in research.
- Do not equate stated preference with underlying need.
- Do not rely only on convenient users.
- Do not ignore non-users.
- Do not listen without changing decisions.
- Do not call empathy complete without burden, power, and systems review.
- Do not claim validation from workshop enthusiasm.
