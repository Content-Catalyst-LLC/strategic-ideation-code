# Sequencing Governance

Sequencing governance prevents strategy from becoming premature commitment, implementation overload, or accidental lock-in.

## Requirements

- pathway purpose statement
- stage map
- dependency map
- readiness gates
- evidence thresholds
- capability-building plan
- capacity-load review
- timing-window review
- reversibility and lock-in review
- ethics and power review
- resequencing rules
- decision-memory record

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not scale before evidence and capability are strong enough.
- Do not treat a timeline as a pathway.
- Do not let urgency bypass governance.
- Do not create technical lock-in before ethical and institutional review.
- Do not overload the teams expected to implement the strategy.
- Do not include stakeholders only after major commitments have hardened.
- Do not continue a sequence after evidence shows the order is wrong.
