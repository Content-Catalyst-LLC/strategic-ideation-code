# Layered Diagnosis Workshop

## Purpose

Help a strategy team distinguish ideation, strategy, tactics, and feedback failures.

## Suggested duration

90 to 120 minutes.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Operational owner
- Analyst or evidence lead
- Stakeholder or ethics representative
- Facilitator
- Decision owner

## Agenda

### 1. Frame the issue

Ask: What problem are we diagnosing?

### 2. Classify by layer

Use four columns:

- Ideation: Is the problem framed correctly?
- Strategy: Are priorities and tradeoffs clear?
- Tactics: Are actions aligned and executable?
- Learning: Is feedback routed to the right layer?

### 3. Identify evidence

Separate evidence from assumptions. Mark contested evidence explicitly.

### 4. Identify tactical translation gaps

Ask whether every major tactic has:

- strategic owner
- decision rule
- implementation pathway
- feedback mechanism
- review trigger

### 5. Decide next intervention

Do not add activity until the layer of failure is clear.

## Output

- layer diagnosis
- recommended intervention
- assumptions to test
- feedback to route
- decision-memory entry
