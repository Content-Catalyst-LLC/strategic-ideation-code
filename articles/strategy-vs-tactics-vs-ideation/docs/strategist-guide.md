# Strategist Guide

This scaffold is designed for professional strategy work. It supports four recurring strategy questions:

1. **Conceptual:** Are we framing the problem correctly?
2. **Directional:** Have we made clear strategic choices?
3. **Operational:** Can the strategy be translated into coherent tactics?
4. **Learning:** Is feedback being routed to the right layer?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Classify the issue

Use `layer_profile_contexts.csv` to identify whether the situation is ideation-heavy, tactically busy, strategically coherent, fragmented, experimental, or operationally capable but conceptually narrow.

### Step 2: Score initiatives

Use `strategic_initiatives.csv` and the Python workflow to compare initiatives by strategic fit, feasibility, leverage, learning value, legitimacy, uncertainty, and assumption risk.

### Step 3: Review tactical translation

Use `tactical_actions.csv` to test whether strategy has actually been converted into aligned tactical action.

### Step 4: Surface assumptions

Use `assumptions.csv` to identify high-criticality, low-confidence assumptions. These should become the basis for testing, prototyping, stakeholder review, or strategy revision.

### Step 5: Route feedback

Use `feedback_events.csv` to decide whether evidence should trigger tactical adjustment, strategic review, conceptual reframing, learning-system improvement, or legitimacy review.

## Professional facilitation questions

- Which layer are we discussing right now?
- Are we generating possibilities, choosing direction, executing action, or interpreting feedback?
- What are we treating as evidence?
- What are we treating as assumption?
- Which tactical activities lack a clear strategic link?
- Which strategic commitments lack tactical translation?
- Which ideas repeatedly fail during selection?
- Which failures are being misclassified as execution problems?
