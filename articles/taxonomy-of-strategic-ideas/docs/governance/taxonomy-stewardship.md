# Strategic Taxonomy Stewardship

Taxonomy stewardship keeps strategic classifications meaningful, retrievable, and ethically responsible.

## Requirements

- taxonomy owner
- category definitions
- controlled vocabulary
- metadata schema
- relationship model
- maturity-state rules
- evidence status standard
- category change control
- retrieval testing cadence
- AI-assisted classification review
- ethics and stakeholder classification review

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not classify all strategic material as generic ideas.
- Do not treat slogans, tasks, metrics, and strategies as the same kind of object.
- Do not let AI tags become authoritative without review.
- Do not classify dissent as mere resistance.
- Do not hide burden, harm, or affected-group experience outside the taxonomy.
- Do not let taxonomy grow without governance, pruning, and user guidance.
