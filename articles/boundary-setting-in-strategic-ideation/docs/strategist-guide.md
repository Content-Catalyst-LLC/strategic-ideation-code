# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports boundary setting through ten recurring questions:

1. **Problem boundary:** What is inside and outside the current problem frame?
2. **System boundary:** What wider structures, feedback loops, and constraints shape the problem?
3. **Stakeholder boundary:** Who counts, who is missing, and who bears burden?
4. **Causal boundary:** What causes are included or excluded?
5. **Temporal boundary:** What time horizon defines success and consequence?
6. **Institutional boundary:** What can the institution decide, influence, coordinate, escalate, or monitor?
7. **Evidence boundary:** What forms of knowledge count?
8. **Ethical boundary:** Who benefits from the boundary and who may be harmed by it?
9. **Option sensitivity:** Does an option remain strong under alternative boundaries?
10. **Revision triggers:** What evidence should change the boundary?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score boundary quality

Use `boundary_frames.csv` to compare problem clarity, system context, stakeholder inclusion, causal adequacy, temporal adequacy, institutional responsibility, evidence diversity, ethical review, revision readiness, and actionability.

### Step 2: Review stakeholders

Use `stakeholder_boundaries.csv` to find affected groups, knowledge holders, implementers, non-users, and future stakeholders who may be excluded.

### Step 3: Compare causal frames

Use `causal_boundaries.csv` to evaluate whether the strategy is acting on convenient explanations or structurally adequate causes.

### Step 4: Extend temporal scope

Use `temporal_boundaries.csv` to test for temporal compression and delayed-effect risk.

### Step 5: Map institutional responsibility

Use `institutional_boundaries.csv` to distinguish authority, influence, consequence, accountability, escalation, and learning flows.

### Step 6: Expand evidence boundaries

Use `evidence_boundaries.csv` to detect metric-only evidence boundaries and integrate qualitative, stakeholder, prototype, scenario, expert, and ethical evidence.

### Step 7: Review ethical boundaries

Use `ethical_boundaries.csv` to evaluate exclusion, burden shift, harm visibility, future consequence, power asymmetry, participation, redress, and accountability.

### Step 8: Test option sensitivity

Use `options.csv` to compare how options perform under internal, stakeholder, system, long-term, and ethical boundary frames.

### Step 9: Detect boundary drift

Use `boundary_drift.csv` to identify unacknowledged changes in scope, stakeholders, metrics, responsibility, or evidence.

### Step 10: Build revision triggers

Use `revision_triggers.csv` to connect boundary evidence to decision rights and response options.
