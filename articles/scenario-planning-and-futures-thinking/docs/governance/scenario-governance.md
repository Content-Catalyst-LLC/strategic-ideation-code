# Scenario Planning Governance

Scenario planning governance ensures that futures work is connected to decisions, monitored over time, ethically reviewed, and preserved as organizational learning.

## Governance requirements

- Scenario planning brief
- Driver map
- Critical uncertainty matrix
- Scenario narratives
- Scenario divergence review
- Strategy stress-test matrix
- Signal monitoring plan
- Adaptive pathway map
- Futures ethics review
- Decision linkage record
- Scenario learning memory record

## Review cadence

- Monthly: signals and early-warning indicators
- Quarterly: trigger conditions, strategy stress tests, and adaptive pathways
- Semiannually: scenario assumptions, driver shifts, and governance adequacy
- Annually: scenario set refresh, ethics review, and learning-memory review

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not treat scenarios as predictions.
- Do not run scenario workshops without decision linkage.
- Do not use scenarios that only confirm the expected future.
- Do not ignore whose futures are included or excluded.
- Do not stress test only preferred strategies.
- Do not let scenarios become static reports.
- Do not monitor signals without assigning owners.
- Do not revise strategy without documenting rationale and remaining uncertainty.
