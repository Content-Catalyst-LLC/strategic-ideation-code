# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports scenario planning and futures thinking through ten recurring questions:

1. **Focal question:** What strategic decision or uncertainty is being explored?
2. **Time horizon:** How far ahead should the analysis look?
3. **Drivers:** What forces are shaping possible futures?
4. **Critical uncertainties:** Which uncertainties are consequential and difficult to predict?
5. **Scenario logic:** How could each future emerge?
6. **Divergence:** Do scenarios differ in strategically meaningful ways?
7. **Stress testing:** How do current and proposed strategies perform across futures?
8. **Signals:** What evidence would suggest a pathway is emerging?
9. **Ethics:** Whose futures, burdens, and interests are represented?
10. **Decision linkage:** What changes because of the scenario work?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score scenario sets

Use `scenario_sets.csv` to evaluate focal question clarity, time horizon, driver analysis, uncertainty quality, plausibility, coherence, divergence, implications, monitoring, decision linkage, ethics, and memory.

### Step 2: Review drivers and uncertainties

Use `driver_uncertainties.csv` to assess impact, predictability, uncertainty, systemic interdependence, evidence quality, stakeholder salience, and monitoring feasibility.

### Step 3: Stress test strategies

Use `strategy_stress_tests.csv` to compare strategic performance across plausible futures.

### Step 4: Monitor signals

Use `signal_monitoring.csv` to evaluate signal strength, noise risk, lead-time value, decision relevance, monitoring quality, interpretation, and ownership.

### Step 5: Design adaptive pathways

Use `adaptive_pathways.csv` to evaluate trigger clarity, reversibility, option value, resource flexibility, capability readiness, governance, stakeholder alignment, and memory.

### Step 6: Review futures ethics

Use `futures_ethics.csv` to evaluate representation, power, burden shifts, intergenerational responsibility, accessibility, accountability, and redress.

### Step 7: Review governance

Use `scenario_governance.csv` to evaluate decision rights, evidence standards, review cadence, signal ownership, trigger conditions, documentation, and accountability.

### Step 8: Preserve learning memory

Use `scenario_learning_memory.csv` to record focal questions, drivers, uncertainties, scenario logic, implications, signals, decisions, remaining uncertainty, and reuse value.
