# Scenario Planning and Futures Thinking Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team explore uncertainty, test strategies across plausible futures, and define adaptive pathways.

## Suggested duration

3 to 4 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Foresight or futures facilitator
- Systems thinker
- Data or evidence lead
- Product, service, policy, or program owner
- Operations representative
- Stakeholder or community representative
- Ethics or governance reviewer
- Decision owner
- Learning-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Define the focal question

Clarify the strategic decision, uncertainty, or domain the scenario work should inform.

### 2. Set the time horizon

Choose a relevant horizon and explain why it fits the decision.

### 3. Map drivers

Identify technological, economic, ecological, social, political, infrastructural, and institutional drivers.

### 4. Select critical uncertainties

Prioritize uncertainties by consequence and unpredictability.

### 5. Build scenario logic

Create internally coherent scenario narratives that explain how each future could emerge.

### 6. Check divergence

Ensure scenarios differ in ways that matter for strategy.

### 7. Stress test strategies

Evaluate current and proposed strategies under each scenario.

### 8. Identify signals

Define early-warning indicators and monitoring routines.

### 9. Review ethics and representation

Ask whose futures are included, who bears burden, and how power shapes plausibility.

### 10. Define adaptive pathways

Create trigger conditions, options, staged commitments, and decision rules.

### 11. Record learning memory

Document drivers, uncertainties, scenario logic, implications, decisions, signals, and remaining uncertainty.

## Output

- scenario planning brief
- driver map
- uncertainty matrix
- scenario narratives
- scenario divergence review
- strategy stress-test matrix
- signal monitoring plan
- futures ethics review
- adaptive pathway map
- scenario governance record
- scenario learning memory record
