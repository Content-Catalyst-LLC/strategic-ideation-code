# Strategic Communication Stewardship

Communication stewardship protects conceptual coherence without freezing language.

## Requirements

- core message owner
- concept definition standards
- controlled vocabulary and usage notes
- evidence and claim review
- audience adaptation rules
- version control
- decision-memory linkage
- implementation meaning review
- feedback and strategic listening process
- AI-use and review rules
- ethics and power review

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not optimize communication only for persuasion.
- Do not allow slogans to substitute for concepts.
- Do not let claims travel without evidence and uncertainty.
- Do not let audience adaptation change the core meaning.
- Do not imply commitments that governance has not made.
- Do not let AI generate fluent strategy language without concept and evidence review.
- Do not remove burden, dissent, or uncertainty to make the message cleaner.
