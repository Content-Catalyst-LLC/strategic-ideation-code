# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports assumption mapping through ten recurring questions:

1. **Strategic idea:** What idea is being evaluated?
2. **Strategic logic:** How is the idea expected to create value?
3. **Assumption inventory:** What must be true for the idea to work?
4. **Assumption category:** Is the assumption behavioral, institutional, technical, ethical, future-facing, or systemic?
5. **Criticality:** How much does the strategy depend on it?
6. **Uncertainty:** How weak is current confidence?
7. **Evidence:** What evidence supports it, and how relevant is that evidence?
8. **Test design:** What test would reduce the most important uncertainty?
9. **Commitment risk:** How costly is being wrong?
10. **Revision trigger:** What evidence changes, pauses, or stops the strategy?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: State the idea

Use `strategic_ideas.csv` to define the idea, expected impact, strategic alignment, reversibility, stakeholder sensitivity, system complexity, and commitment size.

### Step 2: Map assumptions

Use `assumptions.csv` to score criticality, uncertainty, evidence, testability, reversibility, stakeholder sensitivity, time sensitivity, and system sensitivity.

### Step 3: Review evidence

Use `evidence_sources.csv` to check reliability, relevance, transferability, timeliness, bias risk, coverage, and interpretation quality.

### Step 4: Prioritize tests

Use `assumption_tests.csv` to identify the highest-value tests before major commitment.

### Step 5: Connect to theory of change

Use `theory_of_change_links.csv` to find fragile causal links.

### Step 6: Design prototypes

Use `prototype_learning.csv` to align prototypes with critical assumptions.

### Step 7: Compare option confidence

Use `option_confidence.csv` to compare expected value against assumption risk, commitment risk, evidence, reversibility, and learning value.

### Step 8: Review stakeholder and system assumptions

Use `stakeholder_assumptions.csv` and `system_response_assumptions.csv` to identify trust, burden, legitimacy, feedback, gaming, and adaptation risks.

### Step 9: Stress future-facing assumptions

Use `future_assumptions.csv` to monitor future conditions and scenario sensitivity.

### Step 10: Build revision triggers

Use `revision_triggers.csv` to connect evidence to governance action.
