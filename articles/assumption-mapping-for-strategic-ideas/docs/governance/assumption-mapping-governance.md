# Assumption Mapping Governance

Assumption governance ensures that strategic beliefs remain visible, testable, and connected to decisions.

## Governance requirements

- Assumption inventory
- Criticality and uncertainty scoring
- Evidence review
- Test prioritization
- Prototype learning plan
- Theory-of-change assumption review
- Option confidence review
- Stakeholder sensitivity review
- System response review
- Future-facing assumption review
- Revision-trigger log
- Decision-memory record

## Review cadence

- Before option selection: map and score assumptions
- Before prototype: connect prototype to critical assumption
- Before pilot: define success threshold and decision if failed
- Before scale: review evidence, transferability, stakeholder risk, and system response
- During implementation: monitor revision triggers
- After implementation: update decision memory

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not treat confidence as evidence.
- Do not test only convenient assumptions.
- Do not label an assumption validated unless the evidence directly tests it.
- Do not scale high-risk assumptions without revision triggers.
- Do not ignore stakeholder or system assumptions.
- Do not let old assumptions persist after conditions change.
