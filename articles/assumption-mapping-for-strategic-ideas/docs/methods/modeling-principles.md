# Modeling Principles for Assumption Mapping

## 1. Treat assumptions as load-bearing beliefs

The most important assumptions are not the most visible. They are the beliefs without which the strategy cannot hold together.

## 2. Separate criticality from uncertainty

A useful assumption map distinguishes what is important from what is unknown.

## 3. Use evidence quality, relevance, and transferability

Evidence should support the specific assumption under conditions relevant to the strategy.

## 4. Test the assumptions that matter most

Do not spend learning capacity testing easy assumptions while critical uncertainty remains unresolved.

## 5. Connect prototypes to assumptions

A prototype is strategically useful when it tests a critical assumption, not merely when it demonstrates an attractive idea.

## 6. Link assumptions to theory of change

Every link between activity, participation, behavior, outcome, and impact contains assumptions.

## 7. Watch stakeholder and system assumptions

Behavior, legitimacy, trust, burden shifts, feedback loops, and metric gaming often decide whether a strategy works.

## 8. Build revision triggers

Assumptions should change decisions. A map without triggers becomes documentation rather than learning.
