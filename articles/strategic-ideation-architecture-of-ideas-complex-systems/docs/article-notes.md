# Strategic Ideation: Architecture of Ideas in Complex Systems

This folder supports the Strategic Ideation pillar with reproducible examples for:

- strategic idea portfolios
- weighted idea scoring
- strategic-fit similarity
- assumption mapping
- revision flags
- option architecture
- prototype feedback
- implementation readiness
- SQL strategy metadata

Important distinction: strategic ideation is not brainstorming alone. It is the disciplined structuring of ideas so they can support judgment, alignment, implementation, and learning.
