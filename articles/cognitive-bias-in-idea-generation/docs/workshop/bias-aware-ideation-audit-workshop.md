# Bias-Aware Ideation Audit Workshop

## Agenda
1. Reconstruct how ideas were generated.
2. Identify anchors and early cues.
3. Review availability pressure and source diversity.
4. Rotate frames across efficiency, trust, access, resilience, legitimacy, learning, risk, governance, and stakeholder burden.
5. Score idea-space diversity.
6. Surface assumptions and power-protective realism claims.
7. Review stakeholder visibility.
8. Design interventions for the next ideation cycle.
