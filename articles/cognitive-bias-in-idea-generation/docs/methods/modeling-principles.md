# Modeling Principles for Cognitive Bias in Idea Generation

1. Treat biased ideation as constrained search.
2. Diagnose the idea space before ranking ideas.
3. Separate generation from evaluation.
4. Score diversity by structure, not idea count.
5. Audit institutional filters, metrics, templates, dashboards, taxonomies, and AI workflows.
6. Include stakeholders during generation, not only feedback.
7. Preserve rejected and deferred ideas.
8. Treat AI outputs as pattern-sensitive and require source-diversity review.
