# Cognitive Bias in Idea Generation

Advanced companion code for the Strategic Ideation article **Cognitive Bias in Idea Generation**.

This scaffold treats biased ideation as constrained search. Availability, anchoring, conformity, framing rigidity, institutional lock-in, expert enclosure, AI-amplified familiarity, stakeholder exclusion, and premature convergence shape which ideas appear before formal evaluation begins.

## Professional capabilities

- Bias-profile diagnostics for ideation contexts
- Idea-space diversity and search-breadth scoring
- Anchoring, availability, conformity, and framing-rigidity review
- Institutional lock-in and metric/filter audit
- Stakeholder visibility and power-bias review
- Premature-convergence and pseudo-innovation risk flags
- Frame-rotation and assumption-surfacing workflows
- AI-amplified familiarity checks for generated outputs
- Intervention design for better ideation conditions
- Decision-memory templates for rejected, deferred, and excluded ideas
