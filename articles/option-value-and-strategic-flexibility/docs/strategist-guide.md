# Strategist Guide

Use this scaffold to ask ten questions:

1. What is being committed now, and what remains open?
2. What unknowns make flexibility valuable?
3. What lock-in risks exist?
4. Which future choices are preserved?
5. What evidence will improve the next decision?
6. When should we expand, revise, pause, or stop?
7. What does keeping the option open cost?
8. When does the option lose value?
9. Who owns monitoring and action?
10. Who pays for flexibility?
