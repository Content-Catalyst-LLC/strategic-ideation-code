# Governance for Option Value and Strategic Flexibility

Option-value governance ensures that flexibility does not become drift, avoidance, or asymmetric burden shifting.

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not call vague delay flexibility.
- Do not run pilots without decision gates.
- Do not keep options open without carrying-cost review.
- Do not preserve flexibility for one actor by locking others into uncertainty.
- Do not commit irreversibly before testing material assumptions.
- Do not treat option value as free.
- Do not allow options to expire silently.
- Do not forget why a commitment was staged.
