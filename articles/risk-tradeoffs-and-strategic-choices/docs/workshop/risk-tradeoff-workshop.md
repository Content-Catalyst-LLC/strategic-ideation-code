# Risk, Tradeoffs, and Strategic Choices Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team decide consciously among imperfect options by making exposure, opportunity cost, time horizon, value priority, system effect, and ethical burden visible before commitment.

## Suggested duration

3 to 4 hours.

## Participants

- Decision owner
- Strategy lead
- Finance or resource lead
- Operations or implementation lead
- Risk or governance representative
- Systems thinker
- Stakeholder or user representative
- Ethics reviewer
- Challenge-role participant
- Learning-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Define the decision

Clarify the decision question, owner, deadline, decision boundary, and status.

### 2. Identify objectives in tension

List goals that cannot all be maximized.

### 3. Compare real options

Include doing nothing, staged commitment, resilience-first options, high-upside options, and lower-risk alternatives.

### 4. Map risk exposure

Evaluate financial, implementation, reputation, regulatory, ethical, systemic, and strategic exposure.

### 5. Surface opportunity cost

Name what each option displaces.

### 6. Test time horizons

Review immediate, short-term, medium-term, long-term, and intergenerational consequences.

### 7. Review reversibility and lock-in

Identify switching costs, ecosystem dependence, contractual constraints, platform dependence, and exit paths.

### 8. Trace system effects

Map feedback, delays, adaptation, externalities, and second-order consequences.

### 9. Clarify value priorities

State which values receive priority when objectives conflict.

### 10. Review ethical burden

Ask who benefits, who pays, who bears risk, who has voice, and what redress exists.

### 11. Create decision memory

Document tradeoffs, assumptions, rationale, triggers, owners, and review cadence.

## Output

- decision brief
- objective conflict map
- option portfolio
- exposure profile
- opportunity-cost register
- temporal tradeoff review
- scenario stress-test matrix
- lock-in and reversibility review
- resource allocation review
- value priority statement
- ethical burden review
- decision memory record
