# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports risk and tradeoff analysis through ten recurring questions:

1. **Decision:** What choice is actually being made?
2. **Objectives:** Which goals cannot all be maximized?
3. **Options:** What alternatives are genuinely available?
4. **Exposure:** What vulnerabilities does each option create?
5. **Opportunity cost:** What is lost by choosing this path?
6. **Time horizon:** How do costs and benefits shift over time?
7. **Reversibility:** How hard is the decision to undo?
8. **System effects:** What second-order consequences may emerge?
9. **Value priority:** Which priorities are being chosen?
10. **Accountability:** Who benefits, who bears risk, and what triggers revision?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score strategic options

Use `strategic_options.csv` to evaluate return, resilience, flexibility, legitimacy, opportunity value, exposure, reversibility, implementation readiness, ethical resilience, and learning value.

### Step 2: Map risk exposure

Use `risk_exposures.csv` to evaluate financial, implementation, reputational, regulatory, ethical, systemic, and strategic exposure.

### Step 3: Surface opportunity cost

Use `opportunity_costs.csv` to evaluate capability cost, learning cost, flexibility cost, preparedness cost, legitimacy cost, innovation cost, delay cost, and visibility.

### Step 4: Test temporal tradeoffs

Use `temporal_tradeoffs.csv` to evaluate immediate, short-term, medium-term, long-term, intergenerational, near-term, and deferred consequences.

### Step 5: Stress test scenarios

Use `scenario_stress_tests.csv` to evaluate performance across stable growth, resource constraint, trust crisis, regulatory shift, system disruption, environmental stress, and implementation delay.

### Step 6: Review lock-in

Use `lock_in_reversibility.csv` to evaluate irreversibility, switching cost, ecosystem dependence, contractual constraint, platform dependence, governance constraint, retained flexibility, and exit path quality.

### Step 7: Review resource allocation

Use `resource_allocation.csv` to evaluate whether budgets, staffing, attention, measurement, reserves, learning budgets, and stakeholder review budgets match declared priorities.

### Step 8: Clarify value conflicts

Use `value_conflicts.csv` to identify tensions between efficiency, resilience, equity, speed, legitimacy, innovation, and control.

### Step 9: Review ethical burden

Use `ethical_burdens.csv` to evaluate benefit concentration, burden concentration, stakeholder voice, transparency, redress, future-generation burden, distributional review, and accountability.

### Step 10: Preserve decision memory

Use `decision_memory.csv` to document the decision question, objective conflicts, options, exposure, opportunity costs, temporal tradeoffs, value priorities, revision triggers, and reuse value.
