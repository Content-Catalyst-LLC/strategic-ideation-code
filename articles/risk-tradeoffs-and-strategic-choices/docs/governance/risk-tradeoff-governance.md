# Governance for Risk, Tradeoffs, and Strategic Choices

Risk and tradeoff governance ensures that strategic choices do not hide opportunity costs, defer burdens, or turn value conflicts into technical language.

## Governance requirements

- Decision brief
- Objective conflict map
- Strategic option profile
- Risk exposure profile
- Opportunity-cost register
- Temporal tradeoff review
- Scenario stress-test matrix
- Lock-in and reversibility review
- Resource allocation review
- Value priority statement
- Ethical burden review
- Decision memory record

## Review cadence

- Before commitment: option, exposure, opportunity cost, lock-in, value, ethics review
- Monthly: resource allocation, implementation exposure, short-term tradeoff review
- Quarterly: temporal effects, scenario stress test, resource coherence
- Annually: decision memory, value-priority consistency, deferred-cost review

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not claim all priorities are equally protected under scarcity.
- Do not present exposure without absorptive capacity.
- Do not choose without naming the path not taken.
- Do not compare short-term benefits against invisible long-term costs.
- Do not optimize away resilience without naming the risk transfer.
- Do not treat value conflicts as purely technical issues.
- Do not shift burdens without accountability and redress.
- Do not forget why a tradeoff was accepted.
