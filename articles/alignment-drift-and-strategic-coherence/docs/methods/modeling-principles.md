# Modeling Principles for Alignment Drift and Strategic Coherence

1. Alignment drift is strategic distance between intended logic and realized action.
2. Strategic coherence is relational, not merely rhetorical.
3. Coherence is not uniformity.
4. Local adaptation is healthy only when it preserves strategic logic.
5. Metrics can become substitutes for purpose.
6. Resources reveal whether strategy is real.
7. Governance must have authority to correct drift.
8. Decision memory prevents unexplained erosion.
9. Ethical coherence must be maintained through implementation.
10. Adaptive coherence allows revision without silent strategic decay.
