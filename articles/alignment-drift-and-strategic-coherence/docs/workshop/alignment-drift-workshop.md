# Alignment Drift and Strategic Coherence Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team determine whether a strategy is still guiding real decisions or whether it has gradually moved away from its intended logic.

## Suggested duration

3 to 4 hours.

## Outputs

- strategic intent reconstruction
- current action inventory
- intent-versus-action drift map
- resource alignment review
- metric and incentive audit
- translation and narrative review
- portfolio coherence review
- governance authority assessment
- ethics and power review
- coherence decision record
