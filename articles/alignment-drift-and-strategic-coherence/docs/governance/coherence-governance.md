# Coherence Governance

Coherence governance prevents strategy from remaining in language while drifting in action.

## Requirements

- strategic intent record
- current action inventory
- drift signal log
- resource alignment review
- incentive and metric audit
- portfolio coherence review
- narrative and interpretation review
- governance authority review
- ethics and power review
- decision-memory record
- revision triggers and stop rules

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not confuse strategic language with strategic control.
- Do not let metrics replace purpose.
- Do not let resources drift while claims remain unchanged.
- Do not let governance become status reporting.
- Do not treat local adaptation as coherent without evidence.
- Do not maintain stakeholder language while weakening voice and redress.
- Do not restore coherence through communication alone when resources, incentives, or governance are misaligned.
