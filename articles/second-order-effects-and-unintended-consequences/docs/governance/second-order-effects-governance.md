# Second-Order Effects Governance

Second-order effects governance connects intervention design to monitoring, learning, burden review, and revision authority.

## Governance requirements

- First-order effect statement
- Propagation pathway map
- Adaptive actor review
- Incentive distortion review
- Feedback-loop review
- Burden-shift review
- Fragility review
- Scenario stress matrix
- Early-warning indicator set
- Revision-trigger log
- Decision-memory record

## Review cadence

- Before ideation: identify first-order goal and known system boundaries
- Before option selection: map propagation, incentives, and actor response
- Before implementation: stress-test burden, fragility, and scenarios
- During implementation: monitor early-warning indicators
- After implementation: review delayed effects and update decision memory
- Before scale: require explicit second-order review and revision triggers

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not declare success after first-order gain alone.
- Do not treat actors as passive.
- Do not ignore burdens outside the team boundary.
- Do not scale before testing feedback and adaptation.
- Do not optimize for metrics without countermetrics.
- Do not allow delayed evidence to remain disconnected from decision rights.
