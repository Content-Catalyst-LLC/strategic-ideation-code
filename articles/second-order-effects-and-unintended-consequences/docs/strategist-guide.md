# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports second-order reasoning through ten recurring questions:

1. **First-order effect:** What immediate outcome is intended?
2. **Propagation:** Where can the intervention travel after the first effect?
3. **Actors:** Who may adapt, resist, game, imitate, or work around the intervention?
4. **Incentives:** What behavior does the intervention actually encourage?
5. **Feedback:** What loops may amplify, dampen, or resist the intervention?
6. **Burden:** Who carries hidden cost, risk, effort, or harm?
7. **Fragility:** What slack, redundancy, trust, optionality, or recovery capacity may be reduced?
8. **Scenarios:** Under what conditions could the intervention become harmful?
9. **Signals:** What early-warning indicators would reveal second-order effects?
10. **Memory:** What should future teams know about assumptions and revision triggers?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score second-order risk

Use `interventions.csv` and the Python diagnostic to compare first-order gains with adaptation pressure, feedback, delay, burden, gaming, fragility, learning capacity, legitimacy, and reversibility.

### Step 2: Map propagation pathways

Use `propagation_pathways.csv` to identify cross-boundary effects, dependency creation, externalities, delays, and monitoring gaps.

### Step 3: Review feedback and policy resistance

Use `feedback_loops.csv` to detect reinforcing loops, balancing loops, policy resistance, and delayed feedback.

### Step 4: Anticipate adaptive actors

Use `adaptive_actors.csv` to identify likely workarounds, resistance, gaming, imitation, trust sensitivity, and monitoring gaps.

### Step 5: Review burden shifts

Use `burden_shifts.csv` to detect hidden costs, administrative load, emotional load, risk transfer, and equity risk.

### Step 6: Assess fragility

Use `fragility_risks.csv` to identify erosion of slack, redundancy, trust, optionality, recovery capacity, and fallback strength.

### Step 7: Stress-test scenarios

Use `scenario_stress_tests.csv` to test whether the intervention becomes harmful under stress, delay, adaptation, or system feedback.

### Step 8: Build signals and learning loops

Use `early_warning_indicators.csv` and `learning_loops.csv` to connect signals to revision triggers and governance response.
