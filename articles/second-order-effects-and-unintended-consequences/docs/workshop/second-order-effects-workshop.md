# Second-Order Effects Workshop

## Purpose

Help a strategy team evaluate indirect, delayed, adaptive, and unintended consequences before implementation or scale.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Facilitator
- Systems thinker
- Implementation owner
- Stakeholder researcher
- Data or evidence lead
- Governance owner
- Decision-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Define the first-order effect

State the immediate intended result.

### 2. Map propagation pathways

Identify where the intervention travels after the initial effect.

### 3. Anticipate adaptive responses

Discuss gaming, workarounds, resistance, compliance behavior, imitation, and trust effects.

### 4. Review incentives

Ask what behavior the intervention rewards, penalizes, makes visible, or makes risky.

### 5. Identify feedback loops

Map reinforcing loops, balancing loops, and policy resistance.

### 6. Review burden shifts

Ask who carries hidden cost, labor, risk, administrative effort, or harm.

### 7. Assess fragility

Identify whether slack, redundancy, trust, optionality, or recovery capacity is reduced.

### 8. Stress-test scenarios

Evaluate harmful and beneficial cascades under plausible future conditions.

### 9. Define early-warning indicators

Connect signals to trigger thresholds.

### 10. Preserve decision memory

Record assumptions, expected effects, warning signs, triggers, and revision responsibilities.

## Output

- first-order effect statement
- propagation map
- adaptive actor review
- incentive distortion review
- feedback-loop map
- burden and externality review
- fragility review
- scenario stress matrix
- early-warning indicators
- revision-trigger log
- decision-memory record
