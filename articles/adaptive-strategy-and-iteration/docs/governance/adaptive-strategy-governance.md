# Adaptive Strategy Governance

Adaptive strategy governance ensures that revision is evidence-based, coherent, ethical, and accountable.

## Governance requirements

- Strategic intent statement
- Critical assumption register
- Feedback signal interpretation process
- Trigger condition matrix
- Experiment and pilot portfolio
- Timing discipline review
- Exploration/exploitation portfolio review
- Systems-impact review
- Decision-rights and revision authority map
- Ethical thresholds and stakeholder review
- Learning memory record

## Review cadence

- Monthly: feedback signals, weak signals, and operational evidence
- Quarterly: assumptions, trigger conditions, experiment portfolio, and timing discipline
- Semiannually: exploration/exploitation balance, systems impacts, and governance adequacy
- Annually: strategic intent, path dependence, capability gaps, and learning memory

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not treat every signal as a reason to pivot.
- Do not treat every plan as fixed after approval.
- Do not adapt without clarifying what remains stable.
- Do not run experiments without decision rules.
- Do not scale before system effects and burden shifts are reviewed.
- Do not revise strategy without documenting rationale.
- Do not let adaptation become a cover for abandoning commitments without explanation.
