# Adaptive Strategy and Iteration Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team revise strategy through disciplined learning rather than rigidity, panic, or drift.

## Suggested duration

2.5 to 3.5 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Product, service, or policy owner
- Operations representative
- Data or evidence lead
- Systems thinker
- Ethics or governance reviewer
- Stakeholder-facing representative
- Decision owner
- Learning-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Clarify strategic intent

Define what remains stable as pathways change.

### 2. Map critical assumptions

Identify assumptions by uncertainty, consequence, decay risk, evidence strength, and ownership.

### 3. Classify feedback signals

Distinguish noise, weak signals, operational signals, strategic signals, ethical signals, and scale signals.

### 4. Define trigger conditions

Clarify evidence thresholds and response paths for monitor, adjust, revise, pause, stop, accelerate, or scale.

### 5. Review experiments and pilots

Check whether experiments test critical assumptions and connect to decisions.

### 6. Assess timing discipline

Review whether the organization is responding too slowly, too quickly, or appropriately.

### 7. Review exploration/exploitation balance

Assess whether resources support current performance and future options.

### 8. Conduct systems-impact review

Look for feedback loops, delays, burden shifts, capacity, incentives, and scale risk.

### 9. Review governance and ethics

Define decision rights, evidence standards, revision authority, ethical thresholds, stakeholder review, documentation, and accountability.

### 10. Create learning memory

Document what changed, what did not, why, what evidence mattered, and what uncertainty remains.

## Output

- strategic intent statement
- assumption register
- feedback signal map
- trigger condition matrix
- experiment portfolio review
- timing discipline review
- exploration/exploitation portfolio map
- systems-impact review
- adaptive governance record
- learning memory record
