# Modeling Principles for Adaptive Strategy and Iteration

## 1. Preserve strategic intent while revising pathways

Adaptation should clarify what remains stable and what can change.

## 2. Track assumption decay

Strategies fail when critical assumptions weaken faster than the organization revises.

## 3. Interpret feedback before responding

Feedback must be classified as noise, weak signal, operational signal, strategic signal, ethical signal, or scale signal.

## 4. Define trigger conditions

Teams should know what evidence warrants monitoring, adjustment, revision, acceleration, pause, stop, or scale.

## 5. Balance exploration and exploitation

Adaptive strategy protects current performance while developing future options.

## 6. Test before scaling

Prototypes, pilots, simulations, and staged commitments reduce uncertainty before large investment.

## 7. Govern revision

Decision rights, evidence standards, ethical thresholds, and documentation protect adaptation from becoming reactive or political.

## 8. Preserve learning memory

Adaptive strategy must record assumptions, signals, interpretation, revisions, rationale, and remaining uncertainty.
