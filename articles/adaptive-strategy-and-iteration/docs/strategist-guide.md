# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports adaptive strategy and iteration through ten recurring questions:

1. **Intent:** What remains stable as the pathway changes?
2. **Assumptions:** Which assumptions are decaying or uncertain?
3. **Feedback:** What signals matter, and what is noise?
4. **Interpretation:** What does the evidence mean, and what does it not mean?
5. **Triggers:** What evidence warrants monitoring, adjustment, revision, pause, stop, or scale?
6. **Experiments:** What uncertainty should be tested before commitment?
7. **Timing:** Is the organization responding too slowly, too quickly, or with appropriate discipline?
8. **Portfolio:** Is exploration and exploitation balanced?
9. **Systems:** What feedback loops, delays, burden shifts, capacity constraints, or incentives are affected?
10. **Memory:** How will learning be preserved for future decisions?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score strategy profiles

Use `strategy_profiles.csv` to compare flexibility, learning capacity, exploration, exploitation balance, coherence, feedback intelligence, governance, systems awareness, and learning memory.

### Step 2: Interpret feedback signals

Use `feedback_signals.csv` to classify signal strength, noise risk, delay risk, decision relevance, stakeholder impact, and systems relevance.

### Step 3: Review assumptions

Use `assumption_register.csv` to evaluate uncertainty, consequence, evidence strength, decay risk, revision readiness, and ownership.

### Step 4: Define trigger conditions

Use `trigger_conditions.csv` to clarify when to monitor, revise, accelerate, pause, stop, or scale.

### Step 5: Review experiments

Use `experiment_portfolio.csv` to check whether pilots and prototypes are tied to critical assumptions and decisions.

### Step 6: Review timing

Use `timing_responsiveness.csv` to evaluate response speed, signal interpretation, noise filtering, delay awareness, cadence, communication, and stability preservation.

### Step 7: Review portfolio balance

Use `portfolio_balance.csv` to evaluate exploration, exploitation, option value, resource discipline, focus, and transition paths.

### Step 8: Review systems effects

Use `systems_impacts.csv` to evaluate feedback loops, delays, burden shifts, capacity, incentives, scale uncertainty, and monitoring.

### Step 9: Review governance

Use `governance_reviews.csv` to evaluate decision rights, evidence standards, revision authority, ethical thresholds, stakeholder review, documentation, and accountability.

### Step 10: Preserve learning memory

Use `learning_memory.csv` to record assumptions, feedback, interpretation, revision rationale, decision traceability, uncertainty, and reuse value.
