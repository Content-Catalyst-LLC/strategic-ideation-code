# Bad-Idea Governance

Bad-idea governance prevents weak, harmful, overclaimed, or power-protected ideas from becoming expensive strategic commitments.

## Requirements

- problem-frame audit
- causal mechanism map
- claim-evidence matrix
- context-fit review
- implementation readiness review
- incentive and gaming-risk analysis
- stakeholder burden and harm review
- power distortion and sponsorship review
- red-team and dissent log
- narrative honesty review
- AI fluency and source verification
- stop rules and revision triggers
- decision-memory record

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not advance an idea because it is polished.
- Do not treat executive confidence as evidence.
- Do not allow institutional support to substitute for strategic merit.
- Do not call implementation concerns resistance without analysis.
- Do not hide stakeholder burden outside the decision record.
- Do not scale pilots without context and transfer review.
- Do not use AI-generated fluency to present immature ideas as mature strategy.
