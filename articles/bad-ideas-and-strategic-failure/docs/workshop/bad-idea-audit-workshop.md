# Bad-Idea Audit Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team identify whether a strategic idea is ready to advance, needs repair, should be tested, should be reframed, or should be stopped.

## Suggested duration

3 to 4 hours.

## Outputs

- problem-frame audit
- causal mechanism map
- claim-evidence matrix
- context-fit review
- implementation readiness review
- incentive and failure-mode map
- stakeholder burden register
- sponsorship and power-distortion review
- red-team and dissent log
- stop rules and revision triggers
