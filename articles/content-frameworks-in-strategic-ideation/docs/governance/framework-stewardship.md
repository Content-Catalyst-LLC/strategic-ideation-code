# Content Framework Stewardship

Framework stewardship keeps strategic content systems usable, proportional, coherent, and ethically responsible.

## Requirements

- framework owner
- version control
- content type inventory
- field schema
- evidence standards
- AI-use rules
- training and examples
- review cadence
- exception process
- reuse and retrieval testing
- ethics and power review

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not confuse a template with a framework.
- Do not use consistency as a substitute for judgment.
- Do not allow content fields to hide uncertainty or dissent.
- Do not let AI-generated output bypass evidence and assumption checks.
- Do not make frameworks so heavy that teams complete them mechanically.
- Do not omit stakeholder burden, tradeoffs, or redress because they complicate the format.
