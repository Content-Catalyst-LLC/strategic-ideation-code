# Model Revision Governance

A professional strategy system should define how mental models are reviewed, challenged, and revised.

## Governance requirements

- Named owner for model audits
- Formal pathway for disconfirming evidence
- Dissent-safe review mechanism
- Stakeholder and legitimacy review
- Decision-memory archive
- Scenario stress-test cadence
- Explicit revision triggers

## Review cadence

- Monthly: review evidence that challenges current models
- Quarterly: compare alternative models
- Semiannual: scenario stress-test dominant models
- Annual: audit institutional artifacts that encode strategic assumptions

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not treat confidence as evidence.
- Do not treat disconfirming evidence as anomaly without review.
- Do not allow one dashboard to define the whole strategic reality.
- Do not exclude affected stakeholders from model review.
- Do not preserve a model merely because it is embedded in budgets, metrics, or routines.
