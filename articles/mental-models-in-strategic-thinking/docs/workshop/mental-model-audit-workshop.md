# Mental-Model Audit Workshop

## Purpose

Help a strategy team identify, compare, stress-test, and revise the models through which it interprets its environment.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Domain expert
- Evidence or analytics lead
- Implementation owner
- Stakeholder or ethics representative
- Facilitator
- Decision owner

## Agenda

### 1. Identify the governing model

Name the dominant interpretation of the problem.

### 2. Map causal assumptions

Ask what the model assumes about cause, effect, feedback, delay, incentives, trust, legitimacy, and adaptation.

### 3. Identify exclusions

Name the people, histories, risks, evidence, and time horizons the model does not see.

### 4. Compare alternative models

Use systems, institutional, probabilistic, ethical, game-theoretic, and participatory lenses.

### 5. Define disconfirming evidence

Specify what would force revision.

### 6. Identify institutional lock-in

Identify dashboards, KPIs, budget structures, routines, or governance processes that protect the current model.

### 7. Decide revision pathway

Assign owners, review triggers, evidence needs, and decision-memory updates.

## Output

- governing model statement
- causal map
- blind-spot inventory
- alternative model comparison
- disconfirmation triggers
- revision pathway
- decision-memory entry
