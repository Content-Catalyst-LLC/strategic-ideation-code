# Decision Governance Under Uncertainty

Decision governance ensures that uncertainty is not hidden, over-quantified, or used as an excuse to avoid responsibility.

## Governance requirements

- Decision brief
- Uncertainty classification
- Frame comparison
- Option portfolio
- Assumption register
- Scenario stress-test matrix
- Option-value review
- Experiment design
- Heuristic and bias review
- Ethical uncertainty review
- Decision rights and accountability record
- Decision learning-memory record

## Review cadence

- Weekly: urgent evidence, immediate triggers, experiment results
- Monthly: assumption risk, scenario signals, bias review
- Quarterly: option value, ethics, governance, and decision memory
- Annually: institutional learning from uncertainty-sensitive decisions

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not confuse confidence with knowledge.
- Do not present uncertain estimates without assumptions.
- Do not treat the preferred forecast as the only future.
- Do not commit irreversibly when reversible learning is available.
- Do not let dissent disappear before convergence.
- Do not use uncertainty to avoid ethical responsibility.
- Do not evaluate decisions only by outcome.
- Do not revise strategy without documenting why.
