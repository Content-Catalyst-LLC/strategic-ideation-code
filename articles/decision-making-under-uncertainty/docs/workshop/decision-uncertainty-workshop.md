# Decision-Making Under Uncertainty Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team choose responsibly when outcomes are unknown, probabilities are incomplete, and future conditions cannot be specified with confidence.

## Suggested duration

3 to 4 hours.

## Participants

- Decision owner
- Strategy lead
- Evidence or analytics lead
- Systems thinker
- Foresight practitioner
- Operations representative
- Stakeholder or user representative
- Ethics or governance reviewer
- Challenge-role participant
- Learning-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Define the decision question

Clarify the decision, owner, timeline, consequences, and decision boundary.

### 2. Classify uncertainty

Distinguish risk, uncertainty, ambiguity, complexity, and ignorance.

### 3. Test frames

Reframe the decision as risk, opportunity, resilience, public value, capability, cost, ethics, and long-term consequence.

### 4. Expand options

Generate robust, reversible, experimental, staged, and participatory options.

### 5. Map assumptions

Identify what must be true for each option to work.

### 6. Stress test options

Evaluate performance across plausible futures.

### 7. Review option value

Assess reversibility, staged commitment, modularity, exit paths, and future flexibility.

### 8. Design experiments

Identify prototypes, pilots, simulations, shadow evaluations, or staged rollouts.

### 9. Review bias and dissent

Identify overconfidence, status quo bias, confirmation bias, availability, anchoring, and planning fallacy.

### 10. Review ethics

Clarify who bears risk, what tradeoffs are imposed, and what redress or revision mechanisms are required.

### 11. Create decision memory

Document assumptions, evidence, uncertainty, rationale, triggers, owners, and review cadence.

## Output

- decision brief
- uncertainty classification
- frame comparison
- option portfolio
- assumption register
- scenario stress-test matrix
- option-value review
- experiment design
- bias review
- ethical uncertainty review
- governance record
- decision learning-memory record
