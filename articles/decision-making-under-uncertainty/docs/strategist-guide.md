# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports decision-making under uncertainty through ten recurring questions:

1. **Decision question:** What decision is actually being made?
2. **Uncertainty type:** Is this risk, uncertainty, ambiguity, complexity, ignorance, or a mixture?
3. **Decision frame:** How is the choice framed, and what does that frame hide?
4. **Option set:** Have genuinely different options been generated?
5. **Assumptions:** What must be true for each option to work?
6. **Scenario stress test:** How does each option perform across plausible futures?
7. **Option value:** What future choices are preserved or foreclosed?
8. **Experiment:** Can action generate evidence before full commitment?
9. **Ethics:** Who bears uncertainty, risk, and tradeoff?
10. **Memory:** How will the decision logic be documented for future learning?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score decision options

Use `decision_options.csv` to evaluate expected return, robustness, flexibility, information quality, exposure, option value, reversibility, implementation readiness, ethical resilience, and learning value.

### Step 2: Classify uncertainty

Use `uncertainty_classifications.csv` to distinguish risk, uncertainty, ambiguity, complexity, and ignorance.

### Step 3: Map assumptions

Use `assumption_register.csv` to score uncertainty, consequence, evidence quality, decay risk, monitorability, and ownership.

### Step 4: Stress test across scenarios

Use `scenario_stress_tests.csv` to test option performance across stable growth, technological disruption, environmental stress, regulatory shift, trust crisis, and resource constraint.

### Step 5: Review option value

Use `option_value_reviews.csv` to evaluate learning value, flexibility, reversibility, staged commitment, modularity, exit paths, lock-in cost, and monitoring.

### Step 6: Design experiments

Use `experiment_designs.csv` to assess learning question quality, reversibility, exposure control, evidence quality, stakeholder feedback, scaling triggers, and decision relevance.

### Step 7: Review bias and heuristics

Use `heuristic_bias_reviews.csv` to identify overconfidence, availability, confirmation bias, status quo bias, planning fallacy, and related decision distortions.

### Step 8: Review ethical uncertainty

Use `ethical_uncertainty.csv` to evaluate transparency, burden shifting, representation, accountability, revisability, precaution, and redress.

### Step 9: Review governance

Use `decision_governance.csv` to evaluate decision rights, evidence standards, uncertainty disclosure, review cadence, triggers, dissent protection, documentation, and accountability.

### Step 10: Preserve decision memory

Use `decision_learning_memory.csv` to document decision questions, frames, options, assumptions, uncertainty, evidence, triggers, revisions, and reuse value.
