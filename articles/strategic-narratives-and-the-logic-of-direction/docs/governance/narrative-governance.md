# Strategic Narrative Governance

Strategic narratives should be governed when they guide priorities, budgets, metrics, communication, roles, stakeholder commitments, or institutional identity.

## Governance requirements

- Narrative owner
- Diagnosis record
- Choice and sacrifice record
- Sequence map
- Role map
- Evidence and performance gap review
- Stakeholder interpretation review
- Ethical and burden visibility review
- Drift register
- Decision-memory archive
- Revision triggers

## Review cadence

- Monthly: review narrative-performance gaps in active implementation
- Quarterly: review stakeholder interpretation and role alignment
- Quarterly: review drift, fragmentation, and slogan substitution
- Semiannual: review diagnosis and choice against evidence
- Annual: review narrative architecture across the strategy portfolio

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not confuse messaging with strategic narrative.
- Do not allow narrative to avoid choice and sacrifice.
- Do not let action contradict the story without review.
- Do not exclude affected stakeholders from narrative review.
- Do not preserve a narrative that evidence has made obsolete.
