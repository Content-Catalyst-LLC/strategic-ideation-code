# Strategic Narrative Audit Workshop

## Purpose

Help a strategy team test whether its strategic narrative is coherent, truthful, directional, accountable, and usable for action.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Communications lead
- Evidence or analytics lead
- Implementation owner
- Stakeholder or ethics representative
- Knowledge architecture lead
- Facilitator
- Decision owner

## Agenda

### 1. State the narrative

Ask participants to describe the strategic narrative in their own words.

### 2. Identify the situation

Clarify what is happening now and why strategy is needed.

### 3. Test the diagnosis

Ask what causes the situation and what evidence supports the interpretation.

### 4. Clarify purpose

Name the value, responsibility, or future the strategy serves.

### 5. Name choice and sacrifice

Identify what is being prioritized and what is being refused or deferred.

### 6. Map sequence

Clarify phases, dependencies, feedback loops, and revision points.

### 7. Map roles

Identify what teams and stakeholders contribute.

### 8. Audit performance gap

Compare narrative claims against budgets, metrics, decisions, and behavior.

### 9. Set governance

Assign narrative owners, review cadence, evidence triggers, and stakeholder review requirements.

## Output

- narrative statement
- diagnosis record
- choice and sacrifice map
- sequence map
- role map
- narrative-performance gap review
- drift and governance register
