# Divergence-Convergence Governance

A professional ideation system should govern how ideas are generated, developed, evaluated, selected, deferred, tested, and remembered.

## Governance requirements

- Mode declaration
- Idea portfolio
- Criteria register
- Constraint classification table
- Stakeholder inclusion record
- Selection-integrity review
- Decision-memory archive
- Learning-cycle register
- Revision triggers

## Review cadence

- Before ideation: define mode and purpose
- During divergence: protect exploration and document frames
- Before convergence: define criteria and classify constraints
- During selection: document why ideas advance, stop, or defer
- After evidence contact: revise assumptions and reopen the process when needed
- Quarterly: audit selection bias and stakeholder inclusion

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not evaluate before the idea has been sufficiently elaborated.
- Do not generate indefinitely without convergence gates.
- Do not use criteria that are undefined or biased toward inherited models.
- Do not exclude affected stakeholders from selection criteria.
- Do not lose decision memory after selection.
