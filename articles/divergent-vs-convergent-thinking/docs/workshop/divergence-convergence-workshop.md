# Divergence-Convergence Strategy Workshop

## Purpose

Help a strategy team expand the idea space, evaluate options responsibly, avoid premature convergence, prevent unbounded divergence, and preserve learning across cycles.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Facilitation lead
- Evidence or analytics lead
- Implementation owner
- Stakeholder or ethics representative
- Knowledge architecture lead
- Decision owner

## Agenda

### 1. Name the current mode

Clarify whether the group is diverging, developing, evaluating, selecting, testing, or implementing.

### 2. Divergence round

Generate alternative frames, ideas, options, stakeholder perspectives, and possible futures.

### 3. Elaboration round

Develop raw ideas enough that they can be fairly evaluated.

### 4. Constraint classification

Separate real constraints from assumed constraints.

### 5. Criteria definition

Define what makes an idea strategically strong.

### 6. Convergence round

Evaluate options against criteria, constraints, evidence, legitimacy, feasibility, and risk.

### 7. Decision memory

Document why ideas were advanced, rejected, deferred, or revised.

### 8. Iteration trigger

Define what evidence would reopen the idea space.

## Output

- mode statement
- idea portfolio
- constraint classification table
- selection criteria register
- selected / rejected / deferred idea register
- stakeholder inclusion review
- decision-memory record
- next-cycle learning plan
