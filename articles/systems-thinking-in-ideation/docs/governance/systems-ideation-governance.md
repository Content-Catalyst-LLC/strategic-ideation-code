# Systems-Ideation Governance

A professional systems-ideation process should govern how teams move from visible problems to structural diagnosis, intervention design, consequence review, staged commitment, and adaptive learning.

## Governance requirements

- Pattern statement
- Boundary review
- Structural-driver map
- Feedback-loop review
- Leverage-point ranking
- Stakeholder and burden review
- Unintended-consequence review
- Intervention portfolio role
- Evidence pathway
- Learning-loop design
- Decision-memory record

## Review cadence

- Before ideation: define pattern and boundary
- During diagnosis: map structure and feedback
- Before selection: rank leverage and review consequences
- Before commitment: stage intervention according to evidence and reversibility
- After implementation: update model, intervention, and decision memory
- Quarterly: review structural intervention portfolio and learning loops

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not confuse symptoms with structure.
- Do not build maps that do not affect decisions.
- Do not treat system boundaries as neutral.
- Do not scale interventions before reviewing delayed effects.
- Do not optimize local metrics at the expense of system behavior.
- Do not let implementation evidence fail to update strategy.
