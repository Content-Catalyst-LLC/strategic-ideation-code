# Systems-Ideation Workshop

## Purpose

Help a strategy team move from symptom-focused ideation to structural intervention design.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Facilitator
- Systems thinker
- Stakeholder representative or researcher
- Implementation owner
- Data or evidence lead
- Governance owner
- Decision-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Name the recurring pattern

Begin with what keeps happening, not only the most recent event.

### 2. Draw the system boundary

Identify what is inside and outside the current model. Expand the boundary if important causes, consequences, or stakeholders disappear.

### 3. Map structural drivers

Identify feedback loops, incentives, rules, information flows, stocks, delays, dependencies, and mental models.

### 4. Locate leverage points

Compare surface fixes with deeper interventions in information, rules, goals, and assumptions.

### 5. Review second-order effects

Ask what the intervention may set in motion: burden transfer, gaming, capacity overload, trust erosion, or adaptive resistance.

### 6. Design evidence pathways

Define what would strengthen or weaken the structural hypothesis.

### 7. Build learning loops

Define revision triggers, monitoring signals, and decision-memory records.

### 8. Decide portfolio role

Classify interventions by leverage level, confidence, evidence readiness, risk, learning value, and reversibility.

## Output

- pattern statement
- system boundary map
- feedback-loop review
- leverage-point ranking
- unintended-consequence review
- intervention portfolio
- learning-loop plan
- decision-memory record
