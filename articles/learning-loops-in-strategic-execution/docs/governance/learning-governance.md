# Learning Governance

Learning governance prevents execution evidence from becoming reporting theater.

## Requirements

- learning purpose statement
- assumption register
- evidence-quality standard
- interpretation discipline
- decision authority map
- revision triggers
- stop rules
- follow-up ownership
- decision-memory record
- knowledge scaling and reuse plan
- psychological safety and candor review
- ethics and power review

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not confuse feedback with learning.
- Do not collect evidence without decision authority.
- Do not run retrospectives without action owners.
- Do not treat metrics as truth without interpretation.
- Do not let lessons disappear into slide decks.
- Do not scale learning only from powerful voices.
- Do not protect strategy from evidence that should change it.
