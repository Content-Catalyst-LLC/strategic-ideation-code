# Prototyping and Rapid Experimentation Workshop

## Purpose

Help a strategy team convert an idea into assumptions, hypotheses, prototypes, experiments, evidence, iterations, and decisions.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Design researcher
- Product or service owner
- User/stakeholder representative
- Frontline or implementation owner
- Data/measurement lead
- Systems thinker
- Ethics or accessibility reviewer
- Decision-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Frame the idea

State the strategic idea and the decision it is meant to inform.

### 2. Map assumptions

List assumptions about users, behavior, trust, operations, technology, incentives, systems, ethics, scale, and feasibility.

### 3. Prioritize learning targets

Rank assumptions by uncertainty and strategic significance.

### 4. Choose prototype form

Select the lowest useful fidelity: sketch, wireframe, mockup, role-play, simulation, pilot, policy trial, or organizational prototype.

### 5. Write hypotheses

State what the team expects to happen and why.

### 6. Define evidence standards

Specify what would support, challenge, or fail to answer each hypothesis.

### 7. Design test conditions

Choose participants, context, timeframe, measures, observations, and interpretation limits.

### 8. Review ethics and risk

Define boundaries for harm, privacy, consent, accessibility, burden, representation, cost, and reputational exposure.

### 9. Interpret systems effects

Review feedback loops, delays, capacity, incentives, scale, and burden shifts.

### 10. Link to decisions

Define whether evidence leads to continue, revise, stop, pivot, scale, or test again.

## Output

- assumption map
- prototype plan
- hypothesis register
- evidence standard
- experiment design record
- ethics and risk review
- systems-impact review
- iteration log
- decision traceability record
