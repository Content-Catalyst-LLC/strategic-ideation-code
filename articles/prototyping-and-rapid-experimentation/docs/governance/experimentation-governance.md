# Prototyping and Experimentation Governance

Experimentation governance ensures that tests produce responsible, decision-relevant learning rather than innovation theater.

## Governance requirements

- Strategic idea and problem frame
- Assumption map
- Learning target
- Prototype fidelity rationale
- Hypothesis register
- Evidence standard
- Participant and context plan
- Ethics and accessibility review
- Systems-impact review
- Decision rule
- Iteration log
- Experiment memory record

## Review cadence

- Before prototype: review assumptions and learning target
- Before test: review evidence standard, participant fit, risk boundaries, and ethics
- After test: review evidence quality, interpretation limits, and user behavior
- Before scale: review systems effects, capacity, burden shifts, and governance
- After decision: archive learning, assumptions revised, and decision traceability

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not prototype without a learning target.
- Do not test what is easy while critical assumptions remain hidden.
- Do not treat executive approval as evidence of viability.
- Do not use speed to bypass ethics.
- Do not infer adoption from preference alone.
- Do not generalize from early adopters without caution.
- Do not scale pilots without systems and capacity review.
- Do not run experiments whose evidence cannot change decisions.
