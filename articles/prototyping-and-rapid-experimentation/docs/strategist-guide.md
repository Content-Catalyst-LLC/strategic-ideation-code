# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports prototyping and rapid experimentation through ten recurring questions:

1. **Assumption:** What must be true for the idea to work?
2. **Priority:** Which assumptions are both uncertain and consequential?
3. **Prototype:** What is the simplest useful representation of the idea?
4. **Hypothesis:** What do we expect to happen?
5. **Evidence:** What would support, challenge, or fail to answer the hypothesis?
6. **Participants:** Who or what context must be included?
7. **Ethics:** What boundaries protect people, data, access, and dignity?
8. **Systems:** What feedback, delay, capacity, incentive, or burden-shift risk must be interpreted?
9. **Iteration:** What changed because of the evidence?
10. **Decision:** Will the evidence lead to continue, revise, stop, scale, or test again?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score experimentation systems

Use `experimentation_systems.csv` to compare speed, cost efficiency, insight depth, user validation, assumption criticality, evidence quality, systems awareness, ethical review, decision linkage, and learning memory.

### Step 2: Prioritize assumptions

Use `assumptions.csv` to identify assumptions that should be tested before commitment.

### Step 3: Review prototype fit

Use `prototypes.csv` to assess whether prototype fidelity, cost, speed, reversibility, and realism match the learning target.

### Step 4: Score experiment quality

Use `experiments.csv` to examine hypothesis clarity, evidence standards, participant fit, risk boundaries, interpretation limits, decision rules, and repeatability.

### Step 5: Evaluate evidence

Use `evidence.csv` to assess relevance, validity, contextual realism, behavioral richness, interpretability, decision usefulness, and limitations.

### Step 6: Validate with users and stakeholders

Use `user_validation.csv` to distinguish behavior from stated preference and include non-users where relevant.

### Step 7: Track iterations

Use `iterations.csv` to document learning, changed assumptions, prototype revisions, remaining uncertainty, and next tests.

### Step 8: Interpret systems impact

Use `systems_impact.csv` to assess feedback loops, delays, burden shifts, capacity risk, incentive effects, scale uncertainty, and monitoring.

### Step 9: Review ethics and governance

Use `ethical_governance.csv` to review consent, privacy, access, burden, representation, redress, and governance traceability.

### Step 10: Link evidence to decisions

Use `decision_linkage.csv` to define continue, revise, stop, scale, and learning-memory decisions.
