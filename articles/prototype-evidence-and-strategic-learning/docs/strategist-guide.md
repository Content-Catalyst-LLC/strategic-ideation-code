# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports prototype evidence and strategic learning through ten recurring questions:

1. **Assumption:** What critical assumption is being tested?
2. **Learning target:** What uncertainty should this prototype reduce?
3. **Prototype fit:** Is the prototype appropriate to the question?
4. **Evidence standard:** What counts as support, challenge, or inconclusive evidence?
5. **Behavior:** What did users, workers, or stakeholders actually do?
6. **Context:** How realistic were the test conditions?
7. **Systems:** What feedback loops, delays, burdens, incentives, or scale risks appeared?
8. **Ethics:** Was the evidence generated responsibly?
9. **Decision:** What decision changes because of this evidence?
10. **Memory:** How will this learning be preserved?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score prototype systems

Use `prototype_systems.csv` to compare assumption clarity, learning-target fit, evidence quality, behavioral grounding, context realism, systems awareness, decision linkage, ethical review, and learning memory.

### Step 2: Map assumptions

Use `assumptions.csv` to identify critical assumptions and gaps in test design.

### Step 3: Review evidence quality

Use `evidence_records.csv` to assess relevance, validity, context realism, behavioral richness, sample fit, interpretability, decision usefulness, and limitation clarity.

### Step 4: Review behavior

Use `behavioral_observations.csv` to distinguish stated preference from observed behavior, commitment, workarounds, abandonment, and burden.

### Step 5: Review context realism

Use `context_reviews.csv` to assess whether the test context resembles meaningful real-world conditions.

### Step 6: Review systems effects

Use `systems_effects.csv` to assess feedback loops, delays, burden shifts, capacity, incentives, scale uncertainty, and monitoring.

### Step 7: Review ethics

Use `ethical_reviews.csv` to evaluate consent, privacy, accessibility, burden, representation, redress, expectations, and accountability.

### Step 8: Review decision rules

Use `decision_rules.csv` to connect evidence to thresholds, stop rules, revise rules, scale rules, resources, and authority.

### Step 9: Review learning memory

Use `learning_memory.csv` to ensure the organization preserves what was tested, learned, decided, limited, and left uncertain.
