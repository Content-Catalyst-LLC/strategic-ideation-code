# Prototype Evidence Governance

Prototype evidence governance ensures that prototypes support responsible strategic learning rather than persuasion, weak validation, or premature commitment.

## Governance requirements

- Decision being informed
- Critical assumption register
- Learning target
- Prototype plan
- Evidence standard
- Behavior observation plan
- Context realism review
- Systems impact review
- Ethical prototype review
- Decision rule
- Prototype evidence memory record

## Review cadence

- Before prototype: review assumptions, learning target, evidence standard, context, ethics, and decision rule
- During prototype: monitor behavior, access, burden, privacy, and unexpected systems effects
- After test: interpret evidence, limitations, alternative explanations, and missing groups
- Before decision: apply decision rule and document rationale
- After decision: preserve learning memory and communicate relevant outcomes

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not prototype without a learning question.
- Do not treat positive reactions as proof of adoption.
- Do not use polished prototypes to hide weak evidence.
- Do not generalize from unrealistic contexts.
- Do not ignore non-users, abandoners, skeptical groups, or workers who absorb burden.
- Do not scale without systems and ethics review.
- Do not collect evidence without decision rules.
- Do not let learning disappear after the test.
