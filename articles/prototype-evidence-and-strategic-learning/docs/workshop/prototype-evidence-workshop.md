# Prototype Evidence and Strategic Learning Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team convert prototypes from persuasion artifacts into strategic learning instruments.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Design researcher
- Product, service, or policy owner
- Data or measurement lead
- Frontline or operations representative
- Accessibility reviewer
- Ethics or governance reviewer
- Systems thinker
- Decision owner
- Learning-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Name the decision

What decision is the prototype meant to inform?

### 2. Identify critical assumptions

List assumptions and rank them by uncertainty and consequence.

### 3. Define the learning target

Choose the uncertainty that must be reduced next.

### 4. Select prototype form and fidelity

Choose the simplest prototype that can answer the learning question.

### 5. Define evidence standards

State what would support, challenge, or fail to answer the hypothesis.

### 6. Plan behavior observation

Identify what users, workers, or stakeholders must actually do for evidence to count.

### 7. Review context realism

Check setting, incentives, support, constraints, participant fit, scale similarity, and time horizon.

### 8. Review systems effects

Look for feedback loops, delays, burden shifts, capacity, incentives, scale uncertainty, and monitoring needs.

### 9. Conduct ethics review

Assess consent, privacy, accessibility, burden, representation, redress, expectation management, and accountability.

### 10. Apply decision rules

Decide whether to continue, revise, pause, stop, scale, reframe, or test again.

### 11. Create a learning memory record

Document assumption, prototype, evidence, interpretation, limits, decision, rationale, and remaining uncertainty.

## Output

- decision statement
- critical assumption register
- learning target
- prototype plan
- evidence standard
- behavior observation plan
- context realism review
- systems impact review
- ethical prototype review
- decision rule
- prototype evidence memory record
