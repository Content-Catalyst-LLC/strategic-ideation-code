# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports complexity-aware strategy work through ten recurring questions:

1. **System type:** Is the environment simple, complicated, complex, or chaotic?
2. **Interdependence:** Which relationships shape outcomes?
3. **Nonlinearity:** Where might effects become disproportionate?
4. **Feedback:** Which loops amplify or stabilize behavior?
5. **Adaptation:** How might actors respond?
6. **Path dependence:** What historical forces constrain present options?
7. **Scenarios:** Which plausible futures would stress the strategy?
8. **Options:** Which moves preserve flexibility and learning?
9. **Signals:** Which early-warning indicators matter?
10. **Memory:** What should future teams know about assumptions and decisions?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score complexity

Use `complexity_environments.csv` to decide whether standard planning, complexity-aware planning, or adaptive scenario strategy is required.

### Step 2: Review uncertainty drivers

Use `uncertainty_drivers.csv` to determine whether uncertainty is mostly volatility, ambiguity, model uncertainty, probability instability, value contestation, or actor reflexivity.

### Step 3: Map feedback loops

Use `feedback_loops.csv` to identify reinforcing loops, balancing loops, delay risks, and burden risks.

### Step 4: Anticipate adaptive actors

Use `adaptive_actors.csv` to review likely imitation, resistance, workarounds, gaming, and strategic response.

### Step 5: Review path dependence

Use `path_dependence.csv` to identify lock-in, sunk costs, standards, trust memory, and transition costs.

### Step 6: Stress-test scenarios

Use `scenario_robustness.csv` to find scenarios that create the largest robustness gaps.

### Step 7: Compare adaptive options

Use `adaptive_options.csv` to select robust, reversible, learning-rich, coherent, and evidence-ready options.

### Step 8: Build signals and learning loops

Use `early_warning_indicators.csv` and `learning_loops.csv` to connect signals to decisions and decisions to revision.
