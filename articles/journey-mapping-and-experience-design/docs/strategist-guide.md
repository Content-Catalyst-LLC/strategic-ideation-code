# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports journey mapping and experience design through ten recurring questions:

1. **Scope:** Which journey are we mapping and for whom?
2. **Sequence:** What happens across time?
3. **Touchpoints:** Where does the person interact with the system?
4. **Transitions:** Where do context, responsibility, channels, or expectations shift?
5. **Friction:** Where does time cost, cognitive load, uncertainty, or burden accumulate?
6. **Trust:** Where is confidence built, damaged, or repaired?
7. **Accessibility:** Who can begin, navigate, recover, and complete the journey?
8. **Behavior:** Where do users abandon, repeat, work around, or seek help?
9. **Backstage:** What internal dependencies produce the visible experience?
10. **Learning:** How will the journey be measured and improved over time?

## Recommended workflow

### Step 1: Score the journey profile

Use `journey_contexts.csv` to compare clarity, emotional confidence, friction, transition quality, accessibility, trust, completion support, backstage alignment, and measurement quality.

### Step 2: Review stage friction

Use `journey_stages.csv` to identify accumulated burden at each stage.

### Step 3: Review touchpoints

Use `touchpoints.csv` to assess status visibility, response quality, emotional support, accessibility, recovery, privacy, and decision support.

### Step 4: Review transitions

Use `transitions.csv` to examine context loss, ownership ambiguity, repetition, status gaps, and user coordination labor.

### Step 5: Review accessibility and dignity

Use `accessibility_dignity.csv` to evaluate language access, disability access, device access, literacy, time flexibility, dignity, recovery, and equity risk.

### Step 6: Review trust and status

Use `trust_status.csv` to assess status visibility, commitment clarity, consistency, explanation quality, escalation visibility, and trust repair.

### Step 7: Review decision pathways

Use `decision_pathways.csv` to examine choice clarity, risk visibility, defaults, timing, cognitive support, consequence clarity, recovery, and behavioral risk.

### Step 8: Review service blueprint dependencies

Use `service_blueprint.csv` to connect visible experience with backstage dependencies.

### Step 9: Prioritize redesign interventions

Use `redesign_interventions.csv` to compare friction reduction, trust gain, accessibility gain, transition gain, feasibility, ethics, and learning value.

### Step 10: Build the learning loop

Use `measurement_learning.csv` to define metrics, qualitative review, revision triggers, and decision memory.
