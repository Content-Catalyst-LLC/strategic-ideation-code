# Journey Mapping and Experience Design Workshop

## Purpose

Help a strategy team understand how a system is lived over time and translate journey evidence into experience-design interventions.

## Suggested duration

2 to 3 hours.

## Participants

- Strategy lead
- Design researcher
- Service designer
- Product or service owner
- Frontline representative
- Accessibility reviewer
- Systems or operations lead
- Data/measurement lead
- Decision-memory owner

## Agenda

### 1. Define the journey scope

Name the journey, user/stakeholder groups, starting point, ending point, and intended outcome.

### 2. Map the current sequence

Identify entry points, stages, touchpoints, transitions, delays, support moments, and outcomes.

### 3. Add lived-experience evidence

Incorporate interviews, observation, analytics, support logs, diary studies, accessibility review, and frontline evidence.

### 4. Identify accumulated friction

Score time cost, cognitive load, emotional cost, uncertainty, accessibility burden, workaround dependency, and dropoff risk.

### 5. Identify transition risks

Look for context loss, handoff ambiguity, repetition, status gaps, and user coordination labor.

### 6. Review accessibility and dignity

Ask who can begin, navigate, recover, and complete the journey without disproportionate burden.

### 7. Connect frontstage and backstage

Map the internal systems, roles, data, workflows, staffing, governance, and feedback loops that produce the visible experience.

### 8. Reframe the problem

Use journey evidence to revise the strategic problem statement.

### 9. Prioritize redesign interventions

Compare interventions by friction reduction, trust gain, accessibility gain, transition gain, feasibility, ethics, and learning value.

### 10. Define measurement and learning

Specify metrics, qualitative review routines, support-signal tracking, revision triggers, and institutional memory.

## Output

- journey scope statement
- stage map
- touchpoint inventory
- transition-risk register
- accumulated-friction register
- accessibility and dignity review
- service blueprint overlay
- redesigned experience portfolio
- measurement and learning plan
