# Analogical Transfer Governance

A professional ideation system should govern how analogies are selected, mapped, adapted, tested, compared, and remembered.

## Governance requirements

- Target problem statement
- Source-domain profile
- Source-target mapping table
- Breakpoint record
- Rival analogy comparison
- Adaptation test
- Stakeholder legitimacy review
- Transfer hypothesis register
- Evidence and prototype plan
- Decision-memory archive

## Review cadence

- Before analogy selection: define target problem
- During source search: compare near, moderate, and far source domains
- Before transfer: map structure and breakpoints
- Before implementation: run adaptation and stakeholder legitimacy review
- During testing: track evidence and unintended consequences
- After decision: record why the analogy was advanced, revised, rejected, or deferred

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not treat surface resemblance as structural fit.
- Do not use prestige as evidence of transfer quality.
- Do not apply analogies beyond defined scope.
- Do not ignore target constraints, stakeholders, or ethics.
- Do not rely on a single analogy for high-stakes decisions.
