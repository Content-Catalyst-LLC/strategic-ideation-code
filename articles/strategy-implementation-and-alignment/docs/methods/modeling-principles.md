# Modeling Principles for Strategy Implementation and Alignment

1. Implementation is translation from intent into work.
2. Alignment is active maintenance, not a one-time state.
3. Resources reveal real strategic commitment.
4. Incentives often outweigh messaging.
5. Culture determines behavior under ambiguity.
6. Coordination matters across boundaries.
7. Feedback prevents drift.
8. Accountability must protect learning.
9. Alignment has limits and can become rigidity.
10. Adaptive coherence is the goal.
