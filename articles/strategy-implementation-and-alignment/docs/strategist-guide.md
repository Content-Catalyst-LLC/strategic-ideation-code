# Strategist Guide

This scaffold supports implementation and alignment analysis through ten questions:

1. Is strategic intent clear enough to translate?
2. What does the strategy require in practice?
3. Does structure support coordinated execution?
4. Do norms and behaviors reinforce the strategy?
5. What does the reward system actually encourage?
6. Is the strategy funded, staffed, and protected?
7. Can teams manage dependencies and handoffs?
8. Can execution generate learning and detect drift?
9. Who owns progress, correction, and adaptation?
10. Who bears implementation burden and who has voice?

Recommended workflow:

- Run `python3 python/implementation_alignment_diagnostics.py`
- Review `outputs/tables/implementation_profile_scores.csv`
- Review `outputs/tables/alignment_dimension_scores.csv`
- Review `outputs/tables/ethics_power_scores.csv`
- Use the generated markdown report for workshop discussion.
