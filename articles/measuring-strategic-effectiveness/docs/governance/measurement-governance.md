# Measurement Governance

Measurement governance prevents dashboards from replacing strategic judgment.

## Requirements

- strategic purpose statement
- theory of change
- balanced indicator set
- leading and lagging indicator map
- evidence-confidence standard
- attribution and contribution review
- stakeholder evidence plan
- metric distortion audit
- feedback-loop protocol
- ethics and power review
- decision-memory record

## Anti-failure rules

- Do not confuse activity with impact.
- Do not let one metric define effectiveness.
- Do not ignore evidence quality.
- Do not overclaim causality.
- Do not hide stakeholder burden.
- Do not measure without decision pathways.
- Do not let dashboards become ceremonial.
