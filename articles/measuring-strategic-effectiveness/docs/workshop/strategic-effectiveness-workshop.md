# Strategic Effectiveness Measurement Workshop

## Purpose

Help a team determine whether a strategy is working, what evidence supports that judgment, what remains uncertain, and what decisions should follow.

## Suggested duration

3 to 4 hours.

## Outputs

- strategic purpose statement
- theory of change map
- indicator coverage review
- leading and lagging indicator map
- evidence-confidence review
- attribution and contribution analysis
- stakeholder and qualitative evidence plan
- metric distortion audit
- feedback-loop design
- decision-gate recommendation
- decision memory record
