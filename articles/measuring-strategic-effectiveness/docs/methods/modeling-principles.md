# Modeling Principles for Measuring Strategic Effectiveness

1. Effectiveness is multidimensional.
2. Performance is necessary but insufficient.
3. Measurement should connect to strategic purpose.
4. Leading and lagging indicators should be used together.
5. Evidence quality should shape confidence.
6. Attribution is often probabilistic rather than certain.
7. Qualitative evidence is necessary for meaning and legitimacy.
8. Metrics create incentives and may distort behavior.
9. Measurement should feed back into decisions.
10. Responsible measurement asks who benefits, who bears burden, and whose evidence counts.
