# Ethical Use of Strategic Ideation

Strategic ideation should not be used to disguise predetermined decisions, exclude affected stakeholders, or convert creativity into performative consultation.

## Guardrails

- Ask who defines the problem.
- Make assumptions visible.
- Include affected communities and users where relevant.
- Distinguish novelty from value.
- Do not use scoring to hide political judgment.
- Preserve dissent and alternative frames.
- Connect ideas to implementation responsibility.
- Keep evaluation criteria contestable.
